Mock Photo Fixtures
===================

These are placeholder entries for the hackathon demo. The app does not open a
camera, read image pixels, call OCR, or call a vision API. The corresponding
deterministic scan outputs live in `data/photo_scan_results.json`.

Expected demo photo paths:

- `data/sample_photos/fridge_demo.jpg`
- `data/sample_photos/pantry_demo.jpg`
- `data/sample_photos/grocery_haul_demo.jpg`
- `data/sample_photos/receipt_demo.jpg`

The files may be replaced by real sample images later without changing the
mock adapter contract.
