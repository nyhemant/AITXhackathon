# Field Trip Kit — Imagine (Image 2.0) prompt guide

**Audience:** busy parents + kids ~2–10 (toddlers → early elementary).  
**Product:** free one-page zoo / aquarium / museum / park missions (no scores).  
**Goal of photos:** kid can point at the subject on paper; face/head obvious at arm’s length; fills print photo block with cover-crop.

## When to enhance

| Case | Tool | Rule |
|------|------|------|
| Real Commons/NPS photo, bad **crop** for print headshot | `image_edit` from original | Keep species identity & natural look; reframe head-forward |
| Real photo too dark / muddy / tiny subject | `image_edit` | Brighten, tighten; no fantasy colors |
| Catalog credit **Illustration · Field Trip Kit** (AI) | `image_edit` or `image_gen` | Same warm watercolor; clearer focal subject; print-fill |
| Park **heroes / maps** | Prefer real PD/CC only | Project policy: no AI park imagery |

## Shared style (append lightly)

Warm, clear, kid-friendly documentary feel. Soft natural light. One main subject large in frame. Calm and curious — not scary, not cartoon-mascot unless already an illustration. No text, logos, watermarks, or UI. Composition ready for landscape print crop with head/face in the upper half.

## Animal headshot edit (template)

```
Reframe this [species] photo for a kids’ field-trip print card: large clear head and face filling most of the frame, eyes visible, subject looking toward the camera or slightly aside. Keep the real animal’s markings and natural colors. Soft outdoor light, uncluttered background. Warm, calm, easy for a toddler to recognize. No text.
```

## Illustration polish (cm / sci template)

```
Polish this Field Trip Kit watercolor illustration for a one-page kids mission print: stronger focal subject centered and large, cleaner shapes, gentle contrast so it reads at print size, same soft pastel watercolor style. Warm family outing mood. No text or logos.
```
