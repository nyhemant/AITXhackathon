Privacy

Checked against the site on October 25, 2026. If something changes, this page changes too.

The short version: there are no accounts and no sign-ups. We count visits with Google Analytics and check page speed with Cloudflare Web Analytics, so we can see which pages help families. A child’s name typed on a hunt sheet stays on that page.

What we don’t ask for

  • No accounts, passwords or sign-up forms.
  • No email list or email box for hunts. If you want to suggest a fix to a place, that note only goes out if you press send in your own email app.
  • A child’s name is optional on a printed mission. It stays on the page and is never sent to us or to Google. We don’t save it.

What we count

KidZooKit uses Google Analytics 4. It tells us things like which pages are opened, which animal cards and places are popular, whether a hunt was made or printed (with its age group and length), which films or cameras were opened, and whether a printed QR code brought someone here. It sees button and menu clicks, but nothing typed into a box.

On animal card quizzes it counts when a quiz starts and finishes, which level it was, and how many questions were right on the first try. That is a number for the whole quiz; the answers a child picks are not sent.

It receives the page address (including anything after a ? or #, such as a QR code tag), the page title, and the usual details any website visit shares, like browser, device and IP address. Google says it doesn’t show IP addresses in its reports.

Page speed and visits (Cloudflare)

The site also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a second counter that is separate from Google Analytics. It measures how fast pages load and counts visits. It sends Cloudflare the page address, the type and version of your browser, and page-load timings. Cloudflare describes this service as cookieless: it says it does not use cookies or local storage for analytics and does not track individual people across websites. That is Cloudflare’s description, not a promise from us. Cloudflare also delivers this site, so it sees the usual details of any web visit, like your IP address, whatever you choose below.

What the counting is for

We use these counts only to see which pages and activities help families and to keep the site working. We don’t use them for ads, to contact anyone, or to build a profile of a person.

Cookies and IDs

  • Google Analytics saves small cookies named _ga and _ga_XB8HKLF4XY in your browser. They hold a random ID (not your name) so repeat visits can be counted as one browser. This is the only ID we found saved in your browser.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics saves no cookie or ID in your browser, according to Cloudflare.
  • The site asks Google Analytics not to use Google signals or ad personalization.
  • The only cookie of our own is one_less_analytics, and it is only saved if you choose the opt-out below.
  • A few settings, like the last map view, a dismissed tip or the “Seen” marks on Watch Live, are kept in your browser and are not sent anywhere.

Videos and cameras

Some Watch Live pages can show a YouTube video or a live animal camera. When one of those loads, that provider gets the usual details a web visit shares.

Turn counting off

Visit kidzookit.com/analytics/off to stop Google Analytics and the Cloudflare Web Analytics script in this browser. kidzookit.com/analytics/on turns it back on. Clearing your cookies also removes the Google ones.

Not found on the site

On the pages we checked, we found no ad scripts, no social-media pixels and no screen recording.
