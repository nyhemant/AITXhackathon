Zoo Day Journal Printable

Draw it. Notice it. Wonder about it. One page to remember the day and keep the questions coming.

1 page · for a zoo, aquarium, or field trip

Print PDF (US Letter) Print PDF (A4)

Print at 100% scale. US Letter for North America, A4 for most other countries. No signup.

What’s on the page

  • Name, date, and place lines
  • A sketch box: draw something you saw
  • “I noticed…”
  • “I wonder…”
  • “It reminded me of…”
  • My favorite animal today
  • A QR code that opens animal cards and talk prompts

How to use it

Before you go

  • Print one page per child and fill in the name, date, and place before you go.
  • Bring a pencil and something firm to draw on.
  • Ask: what do you hope to see today?

During the visit

  • Stop at one animal for a few minutes and sketch it. Wobbly drawings are perfect.
  • Say an “I noticed” out loud first, then write or dictate it.
  • Look, don’t touch, and stay with your grown-up.

After

  • Finish “I wonder…” at home and look for the answer together.
  • Fill in “My favorite animal today” and talk about why.
  • Keep the pages together for a memory book of every visit.
