Animal Bingo Printable

Six different bingo cards plus a blank grid. Spot it, tick it, and get five in a row.

Ages 5–10 · 15–30 min · 6 cards + blank grid

Print PDF (US Letter) Print PDF (A4)

Print at 100% scale. US Letter for North America, A4 for most other countries. No signup.

What’s on the cards

  • Six different 5×5 cards (Card 1 of 6 to Card 6 of 6), so players don’t share the same grid
  • Squares like Stripes, Spots, Long neck, Whiskers, Big ears, Feathers, Scales and Horns or antlers
  • Action squares: Sleeping, Yawning, Climbing, Splashing, Eating, Making a sound
  • Size and color squares: Very big, Very small, Orange, Brown, Black and white
  • A blank grid to fill in your own squares
  • A QR code on every card that opens animal cards

How to use it

Before you go

  • Print the six cards and hand each player a different one.
  • Use the blank grid for a younger child: fill it with 5–6 easy things like “Four legs” or “Fur”.
  • Agree on the rule first: one animal can tick only one square.

During the visit

  • Mark each square you spot. Five in a row across, down, or corner to corner wins.
  • Say what you saw out loud: “Big ears, the elephant!” It turns spotting into talking.
  • Look, don’t touch, and give animals a quiet distance.

After

  • Swap cards and play a second round, or fill a full card for a blackout.
  • Pick the animal behind your winning square and look it up on its animal card.
  • Make your own card on the blank grid for next time.

Before you print

Can a child who can’t read yet play bingo?

Only with a reader. The squares are words, not pictures, and the cards are labeled for ages 5 to 10. A grown-up can read the squares aloud as you walk. For the youngest, fill the blank grid with just 5 or 6 easy squares, like “Four legs” or “Fur”, and play those together.

Will the bingo cards print in black and white?

Yes. Each square is dark text in a white box, and the title band prints as dark gray; the QR code on each card still scans from a grayscale copy. The PDF has 8 pages (six cards, a blank grid and a cut-out word bank), so print only the pages you need.

How many cards do I print for a group?

There are six different cards. For up to six players, print the set once so nobody shares a grid. For a bigger group, print a second set and pair players up, or play in small teams.

What if we never see something on a square?

Squares describe features and actions, like Stripes or Sleeping, not one particular animal, so lots of animals can fill each one. If a square never comes up, skip it: you only need five in a row, not a full card.

What does the QR code on the cards open?

It opens the KidZooKit animal cards on a phone, so you can look up the animal behind your winning square. No app and no signup.
