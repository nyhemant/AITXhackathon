Printing tips

  • Pick US Letter (North America) or A4 (most other countries). Print at 100% scale (do not shrink to fit).
  • Each page has a QR code. Scan it with a phone camera to open animal cards and talk prompts.
  • Want more? Try the virtual field trip or browse animal cards.
