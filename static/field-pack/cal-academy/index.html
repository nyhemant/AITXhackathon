🌿 San Francisco Science Museum Scavenger Hunt: California Academy of Sciences

Printable scavenger hunt for California Academy of Sciences

An 11-stop hunt for California Academy of Sciences in San Francisco. Plan on half day; print it before you go, then tick boxes as kids find each stop.

Trip journal

Ready for ages 5–8, half day.

US Letter or A4 · black & white is fine

  1. ☐Osher Rainforest — Four-story living rainforest dome — look for birds and butterflies. · Rainforest
  2. ☐Shark — Sharks in the Philippine Coral Reef and main tanks. · Aquarium
  3. ☐Penguin — Penguins just outside/near the academy path. · African Penguin exhibit
  4. ☐Jellyfish — Jellies in glowing corridors. · Aquarium
  5. ☐Octopus — Octopus hideouts along the tanks. · Aquarium
  6. ☐Stingray — Rays in shallow views. · Aquarium
  7. ☐Morrison Planetarium — Morrison Planetarium show if ticketed. · Planetarium
  8. ☐Hands-on lab — Touch specimens and discovery carts when open. · Naturalist Center / floors
  9. ☐Seahorse — Seahorses in smaller tanks. · Aquarium
  10. ☐Clownfish — Bright reef fish under the rainforest. · Coral reef
  11. ☐Philippine Coral Reef — Look down: rays swim under your feet. · Steinhart Aquarium

Bonus challenges

  1. ☐Osher Rainforest: look up four levels — what’s living highest?
  2. ☐African Hall penguins: end-of-hall classic — dive or waddle?
  3. ☐Steinhart Aquarium: shark cruise past the glass twice
  4. ☐Jellies: hypnotic pulse — match one beat with a finger tap
  5. ☐Morrison Planetarium: note a showtime even if you skip the show
  6. ☐Find an amphibian or swampy corner — damp life counts
  • Timehalf day
  • TicketsPlanetarium is timed/extra; aquarium+rainforest are the free-flow hits.
  • Get thereGolden Gate Park; busy weekends.

Looking for a science museum scavenger hunt in San Francisco? The California Academy of Sciences has an aquarium, a planetarium, a rainforest and a natural history museum under one roof. Print the hunt below and tick off the Osher Rainforest, the Philippine Coral Reef, the penguins and the Morrison Planetarium.
