Visiting California Academy of Sciences with kids does not have to mean wandering until everyone is tired. This free one-page scavenger hunt for San Francisco, California keeps the day focused and fun.

Instead of racing through every exhibit, start with top picks like Rainforest dome, Aquarium zone, Planet / space hall, Hands-on lab, Dinosaur hall, and Jellyfish. That keeps toddlers and school-age kids engaged without overwhelm.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find something taller than you; Spot a model, bone, or real artifact; Try one hands-on activity; Read a kid label out loud. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Practical tip for San Francisco: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.
