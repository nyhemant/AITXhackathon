Visiting American Museum of Natural History with kids does not have to mean wandering until everyone is tired. This free one-page scavenger hunt for New York, New York keeps the day focused and fun.

Your printable kit focuses on Dinosaur hall, Animal / mammal hall, Planet / space hall, Hands-on lab, Rainforest dome, and Two-toed sloth — a manageable set for young kids through about age 10–11, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find something taller than you; Spot a model, bone, or real artifact; Try one hands-on activity; Read a kid label out loud. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Weather or crowds in NY can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.

Fossils first, then the Milstein hall whale, then planetarium if timed tickets allow. Field Trip Kit is free to use. Open the full interactive list when you want optional Q&A cards, or use the one-page hunt alone for a lighter outing.
