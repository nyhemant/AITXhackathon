🐠 Shedd Aquarium is a classic aquarium stop in Chicago, Illinois. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

Your printable kit focuses on Shark, Stingray, Seahorse, Jellyfish, Clownfish, and Sea turtle — a manageable set for Pre-K through about grade 5, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find something with fins; Spot a flat swimmer (like a ray); Find a shell or hard cover; Watch bubbles or moving water. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

For Shedd Aquarium, arrive with the one-page hunt in your bag, pick a first target from the shortlist, and treat the rest as bonus — a calm day beats a complete checklist.

Kid shortlist at Shedd Aquarium

Crawlable list of top animals featured in this kit:

  • 🦈 Shark — Ocean hunter with a strong swishy body.
  • 射线 Stingray — Flat swimmer — often in touch pools (gentle hands!).
  • 🌊 Seahorse — Tiny upright swimmer that holds on with its tail.
  • 🎐 Jellyfish — Soft drifter — no bones, glows and pulses in the water.
  • 🐠 Clownfish — Bright reef fish — friends with anemones.
  • 🐢 Sea turtle — Shelled ocean traveler — flippers for swimming.
