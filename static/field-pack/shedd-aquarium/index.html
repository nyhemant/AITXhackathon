🦈 Shark

Sharks cruise the big reef windows.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why do sharks have so many teeth? Do they ever sleep?
Teeth fall out and new ones grow in. Sharks rest, but not like we sleep in a bed.

射线 Stingray

Flat rays glide over the sand.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Both
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why is a stingray so flat? Where is its mouth?
A flat body hides on the sand. The mouth is on the bottom, so it can eat what it finds there.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Both, Meat
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?

Why can a jellyfish sting? Does it have a brain?
The sting catches food. A jellyfish has no brain — just a simple body that drifts and pulses.

🐢 Sea turtle

Sea turtles share the circular reef tank.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Plants / leaves, Both
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why do sea turtles have flippers instead of feet?
Flippers are for swimming far. Feet would be better on land, but sea turtles live in the water.

🐙 Octopus

Look for an octopus that can vanish in plain sight.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Hide, Swim, Climb
How does an octopus hide? Why eight arms?
It can change color and squeeze into tiny holes. Eight arms grab food and feel the way.

🐠 Clownfish

Bright little reef fish for a color hunt.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Both, Plants / leaves
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why do clownfish have orange-and-white stripes? Why hide in those wiggly tentacles?
The stripes help them belong on the reef. They hide in a sea anemone. Those tentacles sting other fish, but a clownfish’s slime keeps it safe.

🌊 Seahorse

Tiny upright swimmers — patience pays off.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Both, Insects
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Hide, Swim
Why does a seahorse swim upright? Who carries the babies?
An upright body and a holding tail fit in sea grass. The dad carries the eggs in a pouch.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Both, Meat
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Hide, Climb
How does a sea star eat? What if it loses an arm?
It can push its stomach out onto its food. If it loses an arm, it can often grow a new one.
