🦈 Vancouver Aquarium is a classic aquarium stop in Vancouver, Canada. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

The shortlist highlights Shark, Stingray, Jellyfish, Sea turtle, Octopus, and Asian small-clawed otter. Each pick has a kid-sized blurb so a preschooler or early elementary explorer can understand why it is cool before you arrive.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find something with fins; Spot a flat swimmer (like a ray); Find a shell or hard cover; Watch something jet or pulse. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Practical tip for Vancouver: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.

Treasure hunt checklist (printable)

Sample finds from the free one-page hunt for Vancouver Aquarium:

  1. Find something with fins
  2. Spot a flat swimmer (like a ray)
  3. Find a shell or hard cover
  4. Watch something jet or pulse
  5. Find an animal with arms (or tentacles)
  6. Spot something tiny and bright
  7. Find an animal that walks on the bottom
