Planning a kid-friendly day at Honolulu Zoo in Honolulu, HI? Field Trip Kit by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of animals so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

The shortlist highlights African elephant, Reticulated giraffe, African lion, Sumatran tiger, Western lowland gorilla, and African penguin. Each pick has a kid-sized blurb so a preschooler or early elementary explorer can understand why it is cool before you arrive.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck, trunk, or horns. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Weather or crowds in HI can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.
