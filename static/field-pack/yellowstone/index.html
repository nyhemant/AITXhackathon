💨 Yellowstone National Park

Old Faithful area — geysers, boardwalks, and one finishable basin loop

  • Timehalf day
  • SettingBoardwalks can be icy or hot steam; stay on paths.
  • TicketsPark entrance pass required; summer crowds — arrive early.
  • Get thereDrive to Old Faithful area; boardwalks from the visitor complex.

Animals you might meet

In the wider park — not the geyser boardwalks. Sightings aren’t promised. Give wildlife lots of space — stay in the car or far back.

🦬 American bison

Iconic valley herds — watch from a car or far pullout.

🦌 Elk

More often in valleys than at the geysers — keep a long, quiet distance.
