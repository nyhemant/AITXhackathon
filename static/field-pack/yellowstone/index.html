💨 Yellowstone National Park

Old Faithful area — geysers, boardwalks, and one finishable basin loop.

  • Timehalf day
  • SettingBoardwalks can be icy or hot steam; stay on paths.
  • TicketsPark entrance pass required; summer crowds — arrive early.
  • Get thereDrive to Old Faithful area; boardwalks from the visitor complex.
