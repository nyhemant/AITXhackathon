Short on time? Start here

Do these 3 in order if you can — enough for a short visit. Tap a stop or Print your mission for the one-page sheet.

1 🧗 🧗 Adventure Tower Climb, crawl, and burn energy in the big tower. 2 🫀 🫀 BodyQuest Journey through the human body — heart, lungs, and more. 3 🦴 🦴 Fossil Frontiers Fossils from Middle Tennessee — dig into deep time.

More if you have energy

More stops if you have the energy.

  • 🔧 Makerspace — Dream it up and build something with your hands.
  • 🌱 Early Explorations — Little-kid zone for ages about five and under.
  • 🪐 Solar System Survey — Compare Earth to other planets — light and gravity.
  • 🚀 Wonders of the Universe — Astronomy up close — look for the Curiosity rover model.
  • ✈️ Physics of Flight — Test wing shapes and how air lifts planes.
  • 🌿 Galactic Gardens — Outdoor learning when weather is friendly.
  • 🌌 Sudekum Planetarium — Sit back for a sky show (check showtimes).
