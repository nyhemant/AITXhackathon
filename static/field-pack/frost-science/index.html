🌊 Phillip and Patricia Frost Museum of Science is a classic science museum and aquarium stop in Miami, FL. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

Your printable kit focuses on Aquarium zone, Hands-on lab, Planet / space hall, Jellyfish, Creative Makery, and Free explore — a manageable set for young kids through about age 10–11, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find something taller than you; Spot a model or artifact; Try one hands-on activity; Read a kid label out loud. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Weather or crowds in FL can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.

About this place: Aquarium + science downtown Miami. Everything here is free to print and share with co-parents or grandparents joining the day.
