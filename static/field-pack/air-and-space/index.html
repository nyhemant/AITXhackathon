✈️ National Air and Space Museum is a classic space center stop in Washington, DC. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

The shortlist highlights Rockets & launch, Space shuttle / orbiter, Astronaut life, Planet / space hall, Hands-on lab, and Outdoor Area. Each pick has a kid-sized blurb so a preschooler or early elementary explorer can understand why it is cool before you arrive.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find a tall rocket; Spot an astronaut suit; See a spacecraft up close; Find a countdown or launch clue. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Practical tip for Washington: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.
