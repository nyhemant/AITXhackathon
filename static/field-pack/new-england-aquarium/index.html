Planning a kid-friendly day at New England Aquarium in Boston, MA? Field Trip Kit by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of animals so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

The shortlist highlights Shark, Stingray, Seahorse, Jellyfish, Clownfish, and Sea turtle. Each pick has a kid-sized blurb so a preschooler or early elementary explorer can understand why it is cool before you arrive.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find something with fins; Spot a flat swimmer; Find a shell or hard cover; Watch bubbles or water move. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Practical tip for Boston: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.
