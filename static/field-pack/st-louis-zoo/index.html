Why can’t penguins fly? Why the black-and-white coat?
Their wings became flippers for swimming. Dark backs and white bellies help them hide from above and below.

More talk

Why are gorillas so strong? Do they live in families?
Strong arms help them climb and pull plants. They live in family groups led by a silverback.

🦛 Nile hippo

Hippo underwater views when open.

More talk

Why does a hippo spend so much time in water? Can it really swim?
Water keeps them cool and their skin safe from the sun. They mostly walk on the bottom — they are not strong swimmers.

Hippo cam at the San Diego Zoo · Hippos at the San Diego Zoo · Open Virtual Field Trip

🦁 African lion

Lions along the cat walk.

More talk

Why does a lion have a mane? Why do they live in a pride?
A mane makes a lion look bigger and protects the neck. A pride is a family that hunts and raises cubs together.

Lion cam at the Smithsonian National Zoo · African lions · Open Virtual Field Trip

🦒 Giraffe

Giraffes above the paths.

More talk

Why is a giraffe’s neck so long? How does it drink?
A long neck reaches high leaves. To drink, a giraffe spreads its front legs and bends down carefully.

Giraffe cam at the Houston Zoo · Giraffe calf at the Houston Zoo · Open Virtual Field Trip

🦩 Caribbean flamingo

Flamingo lagoon pink.

More talk

Why do flamingos have long legs? Why stand on one?
Long legs let them wade and find food. Standing on one leg rests the other and can help them stay warm.
