🦈 Shark (whale shark)

Whale-shark-scale wow in the huge tank.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why do sharks have so many teeth? Do they ever sleep?
Teeth fall out and new ones grow in. Sharks rest, but not like we sleep in a bed.

射线 Stingray

Rays.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Both
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why is a stingray so flat? Where is its mouth?
A flat body hides on the sand. The mouth is on the bottom, so it can eat what it finds there.

🎐 Jellyfish

Jellies.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Both, Meat
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why can a jellyfish sting? Does it have a brain?
The sting catches food. A jellyfish has no brain — just a simple body that drifts and pulses.

🐢 Sea turtle

Turtles.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Plants / leaves, Both
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why do sea turtles have flippers instead of feet?
Flippers are for swimming far. Feet would be better on land, but sea turtles live in the water.

🐙 Octopus

Octopus.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Hide, Swim, Climb
How does an octopus hide? Why eight arms?
It can change color and squeeze into tiny holes. Eight arms grab food and feel the way.

🐍 Eel

Long snaky fish — loves rocky hidey-holes.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Hide, Swim
Why does an eel look like a snake? Where does it hide, and why?
A long body slips into holes in the reef. It hides to stay safe and to surprise food.

🦀 Crab

Sideways walker with a hard shell and pinchers.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Both, Meat
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Hide, Climb
Why do crabs walk sideways? What’s the shell for?
Their legs bend that way, so sideways is easiest. The shell is armor. When they grow, they crawl out and make a new one.
