Animals you might meet

On the Pacific coast — not the Hoh rainforest. Sightings aren’t promised. Give wildlife lots of space — look, don’t touch, and stay far back.

🦦 Sea otter

Sometimes offshore in coast kelp — watch from land and stay far back.

🌿 Kelp forest

Coast kelp beds — not the Hoh rainforest. Watch from shore and stay far back.

⭐ Sea star

Ochre stars in coast tidepools — look, don’t touch, and never promised.

🦀 Crab

Tidepools on the Pacific coast — look, don’t touch, and never promised.

🐙 Octopus

Sometimes in deeper water or a rare tidepool — look, don’t touch.
