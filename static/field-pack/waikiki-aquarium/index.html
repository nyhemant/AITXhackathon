Planning a kid-friendly day at Waikiki Aquarium in Honolulu, HI? Baby's Day Out by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of animals so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

Instead of racing through every exhibit, start with top picks like Shark, Stingray, Seahorse, Jellyfish, Clownfish, and Sea turtle. That keeps toddlers and school-age kids engaged without overwhelm.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find something with fins; Spot a flat swimmer; Find a shell or hard cover; Watch bubbles or water move. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Kid shortlist at Waikiki Aquarium

Crawlable list of top animals featured in this kit:

  • 🦈 Shark — Ocean hunter with a strong swishy body.
  • 射线 Stingray — Flat swimmer — often in touch pools (gentle hands!).
  • 🌊 Seahorse — Tiny upright swimmer that holds on with its tail.
  • 🎐 Jellyfish — Soft drifter — no bones, glows and pulses in the water.
  • 🐠 Clownfish — Bright reef fish — friends with anemones.
  • 🐢 Sea turtle — Shelled ocean traveler — flippers for swimming.
