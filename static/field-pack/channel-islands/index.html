Animals you might meet

In the kelp and tidepools — if you get on the water or an island landing. Sightings aren’t promised. Look, don’t touch, and stay far from wildlife.

🌿 Kelp forest

Giant kelp around the islands — usually from a boat or snorkel. Never promised.

⭐ Sea star

Tidepools and kelp — if you land. Look, don’t touch.

🦀 Crab

Tidepools and kelp — if you land or get on the water. Look, don’t touch.
