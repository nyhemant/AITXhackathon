French Island — Tankerton ferry day; koalas never promised

  • Timehalf day
  • SettingMosquitoes; limited medical help on the island; pack water.
  • TicketsPark free; Western Port ferry tickets separate.
  • Get therePassenger ferry from Stony Point to Tankerton; services can cancel.
