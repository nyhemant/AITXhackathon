Visiting Smithsonian National Zoo with kids does not have to mean wandering until everyone is tired. This free one-page scavenger hunt for Washington, DC keeps the day focused and fun.

Your printable kit focuses on Giant panda, Asian small-clawed otter, Red panda, African elephant, African lion, and Sumatran tiger — a manageable set for young kids through about age 10–11, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck, trunk, or horns. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Practical tip for Washington: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.

Asia Trail pandas first, then elephants, big cats, and ape house icons. Field Trip Kit is free to use. Open the full interactive list when you want optional Q&A cards, or use the one-page hunt alone for a lighter outing.

Treasure hunt checklist (printable)

Sample finds from the free one-page hunt for Smithsonian National Zoo:

  1. Find an animal taller than a grown-up
  2. Spot stripes or spots
  3. Watch something swim or splash
  4. Find a long neck, trunk, or horns
  5. Hear a loud sound (or a quiet one)
  6. Point to where an animal might sleep
  7. Find a bird with bright feathers
  8. Photo your favorite animal
