Visiting Point Defiance Zoo & Aquarium with kids does not have to mean wandering until everyone is tired. This free one-page scavenger hunt for Tacoma, WA keeps the day focused and fun.

Your printable kit focuses on African elephant, Sumatran tiger, Shark, Jellyfish, Reticulated giraffe, and Sea turtle — a manageable set for Pre-K through about grade 5, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck, trunk, or horns. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Practical tip for Tacoma: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.
