Home

Where do wild African penguins live?

African penguins live along southern African coasts, such as South Africa and Namibia — not Antarctica.

2

Flight

Can an African penguin fly in the air?

Like all penguins, African penguins cannot fly in the air. Their wings are stiff flippers they use to swim.

3

Food

What do African penguins mostly eat?

They hunt in the sea. Wikipedia says they feed primarily on fish and squid, plus other small sea animals.

4

Voice

Why is the African penguin sometimes called a “jackass penguin”?

One nickname is “jackass penguin,” from the species’ loud, donkey-like call.

5

Colony

What do you call a large group of African penguins living together?

African penguins live and breed together in a large group called a colony.

6

Eggs

How do African penguins have babies?

African penguins lay eggs. They do not give birth to live young.

7

Coat

How does an African penguin’s black-and-white coat help it hide?

The dark back blends with the water from above, and the pale belly is harder to see from below. That hiding trick is called countershading.

8

Flippers

What are an African penguin’s wings like?

The wings are stiffened and flattened into flippers, made for swimming — not for flying in the air.

9

Nest

Where do African penguins usually nest?

They nest in burrows or under rocks and bushes on the shore — not on ice.

10

Myth buster

Do all penguins live on cold ice?

Not every penguin lives somewhere icy. African penguins live on warmer coasts in southern Africa.
