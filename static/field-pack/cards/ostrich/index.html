1

Biggest bird

What kind of living bird is the ostrich?

Wikipedia says the ostrich is the world’s largest living bird — tallest and heaviest. We do not lock exact height or weight.

2

Run, don’t fly

Can ostriches fly?

Wikipedia says ostriches are flightless. Their strong legs are built for running, not flying.

3

Africa home

Where do wild ostriches live?

Wikipedia places wild ostriches in Africa on open grassland and savanna. They are not Antarctic or North American forest birds.

4

Long lookout

How do a long neck and long legs help an ostrich?

Wikipedia notes the ostrich’s long neck and long legs. Standing tall helps it look out and spot danger.

5

Boy / girl look

How can you often tell a male ostrich from a female?

Wikipedia says adult males are mostly black with white wing and tail feathers; females are greyish-brown.

6

Plant-first snacks

What do ostriches eat first?

Wikipedia says ostriches mostly eat plants such as seeds, grass, shrubs, and fruit. They may take small animals sometimes. Plants come first.

7

Giant eggs

What is special about an ostrich egg?

Wikipedia says ostriches lay the largest eggs of any living bird. We keep exact size and weight soft.

8

Chick

What is a baby ostrich called, and what does it look like?

Wikipedia calls a baby ostrich a chick. Chicks are fawn-colored with dark spots.

9

Soft care

How are wild ostriches doing today?

Wikipedia says wild ostriches are less widespread than they once were. Many now live on farms and in protected places. We do not lock a status letter — that grade is a snapshot and can change.

10

Myth buster — sand?

Do ostriches bury their heads in the sand?

Wikipedia says ostriches do not bury their heads in the sand. When they hide, they may press the head and neck flat to the ground so they look like a mound.
