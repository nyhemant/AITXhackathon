🦬 American bison

Huge grassland icon — never approach; watch from a car or far pullout.

Quick tips (Junior Ranger) — tap to open
  • Big grassland grazers of North America
  • Tall shoulder hump + shaggy coat
  • Both males and females have horns
  • Live in herds on the prairie
  • Often nicknamed “buffalo,” but they’re bison
1

Prairie home

Where do American bison live in the wild?

Wikipedia says American bison live in river valleys and on prairies and plains. Typical habitat is open or semiopen grassland.

2

Shoulder hump

What makes the front of an American bison look so big?

Wikipedia describes massive heads and forequarters. The tall shoulder hump is the bison’s famous front-end shape.

3

Coat change

How does an American bison’s coat change with the seasons?

Wikipedia says a bison has a shaggy, long, dark-brown winter coat and a lighter-weight, lighter-brown summer coat.

4

Horns

Who has horns, and what are they for?

Wikipedia says both sexes have short, curved horns. They use them for defense and for status inside the herd.

5

Graze and cud

What do American bison mostly eat?

Wikipedia says bison are herbivores that graze on the grasses and sedges of the North American prairies. Their day includes grazing, resting, and cud chewing.

6

Herd life

How do bison herds usually work?

Wikipedia says female bison live in maternal herds with their young. Older males often live apart and usually join females in breeding season.

7

Reddish calves

What color are new bison calves?

Wikipedia says a calf is reddish-brown, and for the first two months calves are lighter in color than grown bison.

8

Wallow

Why do bison roll in dust or mud wallows?

Wikipedia says bison roll in shallow dust or mud wallows. Ideas include relief from biting insects and play.

9

Soft prairie care

How should people care around wild bison and their home?

Wikipedia says bison can look slow but can run fast, and close encounters can be dangerous. Soft care is space plus a healthy prairie home — no status letter.

10

Myth buster

Are American bison true buffalo?

Wikipedia says they are commonly called American buffalo, but that should not be confused with true buffalo. True buffalo live in Africa and Asia.

Facts from Wikipedia, American bison. Where sources disagree on exact numbers, we keep them approximate.
