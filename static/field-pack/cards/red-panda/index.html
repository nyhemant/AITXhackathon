1

Not a giant panda

Is a red panda a kind of bear, like a giant panda?

Wikipedia says the red panda is not closely related to the giant panda, which is a bear. Sharing the name “panda” does not make them close cousins.

2

Mountain forests

Where do wild red pandas live?

Wikipedia says the red panda is native to the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China. It lives in mountain forests with lots of bamboo.

3

Bamboo eater

What do red pandas mainly eat?

Wikipedia says the red panda feeds mainly on bamboo shoots and leaves. It may also eat fruits and blossoms.

4

Ringed tail

What is a red panda’s tail like?

Wikipedia describes a ringed tail. The bushy tail has red and buff ring patterns.

5

Tree climber

How do red pandas spend much of their time?

Wikipedia says the red panda is largely arboreal. It is well adapted to climbing and spends much of its time in trees.

6

False thumb

What special “thumb” helps a red panda hold bamboo?

Wikipedia says the forepaws have a “false thumb” — a wrist bone that helps the animal grip bamboo stalks.

7

Rusty red coat

What does a red panda’s coat look like?

Wikipedia describes dense reddish-brown fur, a black belly and legs, and white face markings.

8

Mostly alone

How do adult red pandas usually live?

Wikipedia says adult red pandas are generally solitary. They are not pride animals like lions.

9

Cub nest

Where are red panda cubs usually born?

Wikipedia says the mother picks a denning site such as a tree hollow, log hollow, or rock crevice, and builds a nest.

10

Myth buster — fox look

A red panda can look a bit like a fox. Does that make it a fox?

Wikipedia treats the red panda as its own animal — not a bear and not closely related to the giant panda. A fox-like face does not make it a fox.
