1

Home

Where do wild Galápagos tortoises live?

They live on the Galápagos Islands in the Pacific Ocean, near Ecuador. That island home is why they are called Galápagos tortoises.

2

Size

How big is a Galápagos tortoise?

Galápagos tortoises are giant tortoises — among the largest in the world. Wikipedia calls them the largest living tortoise.

3

Age

How long can a Galápagos tortoise live?

Wild Galápagos tortoises can live over 100 years. They are among the longest-lived animals.

4

Food

What do Galápagos tortoises eat?

They are plant-eaters. Wikipedia lists grasses, leaves, and cactus among the plants they nibble.

5

Shell

What is the hard covering on a Galápagos tortoise’s back?

The hard covering on the back is a shell. It protects the tortoise’s body.

6

Speed

How does a Galápagos tortoise usually move?

Galápagos tortoises walk slowly. They are famous for being slow and steady.

7

Eggs

How do baby Galápagos tortoises start life?

Mothers bury eggs in a nest in the ground. Later, the babies hatch and dig out.

8

Hide

What can a Galápagos tortoise do when it is frightened?

When frightened, it can pull its head, neck, and legs inside the shell for safety.

9

Neck

What is special about a Galápagos tortoise’s neck?

Many Galápagos tortoises have a long neck that can stretch up to reach leaves and cactus.

10

Myth buster

Can a Galápagos tortoise leave its shell?

The shell is part of the tortoise’s skeleton, not a coat. It cannot leave its shell.
