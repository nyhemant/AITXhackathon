Quick tips (Junior Ranger) — tap to open
  • They have bold black-and-white stripes — each zebra’s pattern is unique.
  • They mostly eat grass (plants, not meat).
  • They live on African grassland / savanna.
  • They live in family groups that can join bigger herds.
  • A baby zebra is a foal.
1

Stripes

What is special about a plains zebra’s coat?

Plains zebras are boldly striped in black and white, and no two individuals look exactly alike.

2

Food

What do plains zebras mostly eat?

Plains zebras primarily feed on grass. They are plant-eaters, not meat-eaters.

3

Home

Where do wild plains zebras live?

Their habitat is generally treeless grasslands and savanna woodlands in Africa.

4

Family

How do plains zebras live together?

They live in family groups — a stallion, several mares, and their young. Those groups can come together to form bigger herds.

5

Baby

What do you call a baby plains zebra?

A baby zebra is a foal — the same word used for a baby horse.

6

Speed

How do plains zebras escape danger?

An adult zebra can run fast to get away from lions and other hunters. We do not lock one exact speed.

7

Horse kin

What animal is a plains zebra a wild relative of?

Zebras belong to the horse family. They are wild relatives of the horse, not a farm pet.

8

Mane

What is a plains zebra’s mane like?

Like other wild horses, a plains zebra has a short, upright mane along the neck.

9

Voice

What contact call is a plains zebra known for?

Wikipedia describes a high-pitched contact call, often called barking — heard as “kwaha” or similar.

10

Myth buster

Are plains zebras just striped farm horses you can ride?

Zebras are wild, not domesticated like horses. They are horse relatives, but they are not farm horses you can ride the same way.

Facts from Wikipedia, Plains zebra. Where sources disagree on exact numbers, we keep them approximate.
