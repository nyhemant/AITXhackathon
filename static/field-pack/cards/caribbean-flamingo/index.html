Score 0/10

1

Colour

What colour are grown-up Caribbean flamingos?

Adult American flamingos — also called Caribbean flamingos — have reddish-pink feathers. Wikipedia also calls them rosy flamingos because of that pink colour.

2

One leg

How do Caribbean flamingos often stand?

Flamingos often stand on one leg with the other tucked up. Wikipedia says this may help them keep warm or rest with less effort.

3

Food place

Where do Caribbean flamingos find their food?

They wade in shallow water — lagoons, mudflats, and shallow lakes — and stir the bottom to find small food.

4

Flock

What do you call a group of Caribbean flamingos?

Flamingos live and fly together in a flock. Wikipedia often talks about flocks of American flamingos.

5

Chicks

What do baby Caribbean flamingos look like at first?

Chicks hatch greyish and fluffy. They are not bright pink at first — that colour comes later from their food.

6

Neck

What is special about a Caribbean flamingo’s neck?

Flamingos have a long, bendy neck. It helps them reach down into shallow water to feed.

7

Nest

What kind of nest does a Caribbean flamingo build?

Wikipedia says the American flamingo lays its egg on a mud mound. Both parents help with the nest.

8

Beak

What is a Caribbean flamingo’s beak like?

The beak is hooked downward — bent in the middle. That curved shape helps the bird filter food from the water.

9

Pink from food

Why are grown-up Caribbean flamingos pink?

The pink comes from colour in their food, such as tiny shrimp and algae. They are not painted, and chicks are not born that bright pink.

10

Myth buster

Can Caribbean flamingos fly?

Flamingos are capable flyers. They fly in flocks to find food. They are not flightless.
