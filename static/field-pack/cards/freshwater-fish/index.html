Quick tips (Junior Ranger) — tap to open
  • Live in rivers, lakes, ponds, and wetlands — not salty ocean water
  • Breathe underwater with gills
  • Use fins to swim, steer, and stop
  • Come in many shapes and sizes (catfish, trout, perch, and more)
  • Need clean fresh water to stay healthy
1

Fresh homes

Where do freshwater fish live?

Freshwater fish spend some or all of their lives in rivers, lakes, ponds, and inland wetlands — not salty ocean water.

2

Low-salt water

How is fresh water different from ocean water?

Freshwater homes have very low salt compared with the ocean. That low-salt water is what “fresh” means here.

3

Gill breathers

How do freshwater fish get oxygen underwater?

Fish extract oxygen from water using gills. Freshwater fish breathe underwater this way.

4

Fin toolkit

How do fins help freshwater fish move?

Wikipedia describes a typical fish with paired fins plus dorsal, anal, and tail fins. Those fins help push, steer, balance, and stop.

5

Many kinds

How many kinds of fish live in fresh water?

A huge share of all known fish kinds are found in fresh water. The exact percent can shift.

6

Scaly coats

Do freshwater fish wear scales?

A typical fish has skin covered with scales that help protect it. Some kinds have fewer scales or none, so this is not true of every freshwater fish.

7

Water temperature

How do most freshwater fish handle temperature?

Most fish are cold-blooded — their body temperature varies with the surrounding water. A few kinds can stay warmer, so we say most.

8

Egg starts

How do many freshwater fish begin life?

Many fish begin as eggs laid in the water. Some kinds give live young.

9

Soft water care

What helps freshwater fish stay healthy in the wild?

Freshwater fish need clean fresh water. Wikipedia notes that habitat loss and pollution can hurt them. Clean rivers and healthy wetlands help them thrive. We do not lock a status letter — that grade is a snapshot and can change.

10

Myth buster

Do all fish need the ocean to live?

Freshwater fish spend some or all of their lives in rivers, lakes, ponds, and inland wetlands. Plenty of kinds never need the ocean.

Facts from Wikipedia, Freshwater fish. Where sources disagree on exact numbers, we keep them approximate.
