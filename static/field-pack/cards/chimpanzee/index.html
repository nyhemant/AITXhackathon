1

Africa home

Where do wild chimpanzees live?

Wikipedia says the chimpanzee lives in the forests and savannas of equatorial Africa, including woodlands. It is not an Antarctic or Australian animal.

2

No tail

What is a quick clue that a chimpanzee is a great ape, not a monkey?

Wikipedia places the chimpanzee among the great apes. Great apes have no tail — a quick clue they are not monkeys.

3

Fruit first

What do wild chimpanzees mainly eat?

Wikipedia says the chimpanzee primarily eats fruit, and also eats leaves and other plant parts. It may also eat insects and sometimes meat. It is not only a meat-eater or only a plant-eater.

4

Tool makers

What are chimpanzees famous for using in the forest?

Wikipedia says nearly all chimpanzee groups have been seen using tools. They modify sticks, rocks, grass, and leaves — including fishing for termites and cracking nuts.

5

Group life

How do wild chimpanzees usually live?

Wikipedia says chimpanzees live in communities. They spend time with others in their group, not as lifelong loners.

6

Tree nest night

Where does a chimpanzee usually sleep at night?

Wikipedia says chimpanzees build nests in the trees to sleep at night. A sleeping nest is usually used for one night.

7

Hoots and gestures

How do chimpanzees communicate?

Wikipedia says chimpanzees communicate with sound, visual displays, and touch. A common call is the pant-hoot, and they also scream and use faces and body gestures.

8

Knuckle walk

How do chimpanzees usually walk on the ground?

Wikipedia says that on the ground, chimpanzees often move on all fours by knuckle-walking — they rest their weight on their knuckles.

9

Closest kin

Who are among humans’ closest living relatives?

Wikipedia says chimpanzees and bonobos are humans’ closest living relatives. We do not lock one shared-gene number. Details of that family split come later.

10

Myth buster — monkey?

Are chimpanzees monkeys?

Wikipedia places the chimpanzee among the great apes. People sometimes call them monkeys, but chimpanzees are apes — they have no tail.
