🐙 Octopus

Eight arms and a big brain — master of hide-and-seek.

PhotosLearn more

Quiz

Learn first — tap to open
  • Soft-bodied sea animals with eight arms (molluscs, not fish)
  • Arms have suckers that grip and help feel/taste what they touch
  • They can change colour (and skin texture) to hide
  • When scared, many squirt dark ink and jet away
  • Almost no hard parts — they squeeze through tiny gaps (the beak is the hard bit)

1

Eight arms

How many arms does an octopus have?

Wikipedia says an octopus is a soft-bodied, eight-limbed mollusc. Those limbs are arms with suckers along them, not the longer clubbed tentacles squid and cuttlefish add.

2

Soft squeeze

Why can an octopus squeeze through a tiny gap?

Wikipedia says an octopus can radically change shape and squeeze through small gaps. Most of the body is soft; the hard beak is the part that sets how small a gap it can use.

3

Colour change

How can an octopus hide in plain sight?

Wikipedia says octopuses use specialised skin cells to change colour for camouflage and signals. They can also change skin texture. We keep the cell names soft for later.

4

Ink escape

What can many octopuses do when a predator comes?

Wikipedia says octopuses can expel ink as a smoke-screen or decoy, then jet away. The ink sac sits near the funnel so they can shoot a dark cloud and flee.

5

Beak and hunt

What does an octopus use to eat, and what does it hunt?

Wikipedia says the mouth has a sharp beak at the centre of the eight arms. Octopuses are predators that hunt crustaceans, bivalves, gastropods, and fish.

6

Den home

Where do many octopuses hide, and what clue might sit outside?

Wikipedia says octopuses hide in dens, often rock crevices or leftover shells. They bring prey home, and dens are often surrounded by a midden of leftover shells and food scraps.

7

Blue blood

What is special about octopus blood and hearts, if we keep it kid-simple?

Wikipedia says octopus blood uses a copper-rich protein, which makes it look bluish. They have three hearts: two pump blood through the gills and one serves the rest of the body. We keep the chemistry names soft.

8

Ocean homes

Where do octopuses live?

Wikipedia says octopuses inhabit every ocean, from coral reefs and tide pools to the seabed and deep water. No species are known to live in fresh water.

9

Many kinds

How many kinds of octopus are there, if we keep the count soft?

Wikipedia says the octopus group has some 300 known species. Sizes run from tiny kinds to the giant Pacific octopus, often cited as the largest. Exact records shift, so we keep counts and sizes soft.

10

Myth buster

Do octopuses have tentacles?

Wikipedia calls the eight limbs arms. Squid and cuttlefish add two longer tentacles with suckers mainly at the club ends. Saying “tentacles” for an octopus mixes them up.
