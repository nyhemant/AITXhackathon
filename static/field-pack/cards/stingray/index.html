  • Flattened ocean fish with a skeleton of cartilage (related to sharks — soft)
  • Eyes on top; mouth and gills on the underside
  • Many hide under sand on the seafloor
  • The tail can carry a venomous stinger used for defense
  • Usually shy — they sting when threatened or stepped on, not when chasing people

Score 0/10

1

Soft skeleton

What is a stingray’s skeleton mostly made of?

Wikipedia calls stingrays cartilaginous fish. Their skeletons are cartilage, not hard bone — the same big idea as sharks.

2

Flat disc

What shape helps many stingrays hug the seafloor?

Wikipedia describes flattened bodies and large pectoral fins that form a broad disc. That shape helps them rest and hide on the bottom.

3

Eyes up

Where are a stingray’s eyes and mouth?

Wikipedia: eyes sit on the upper surface; the mouth is on the ventral (underside). Gill openings are also on the underside.

4

Sand hide

How do many bottom-living stingrays hide?

Wikipedia: flattened bodies help them conceal in sand. They agitate sand and hide beneath it, often with only eyes and tail visible.

5

Stinger

What is the famous “sting” on many stingrays?

Wikipedia: the stinger (spinal blade) sits on the tail and can deliver venom. Rays may have one, two, or three blades. Soft on exact lengths.

6

Crush food

What do many stingrays eat on the seafloor?

Wikipedia: most feed mainly on mollusks and crustaceans, and sometimes small fish. Flat teeth help crush hard-shelled prey.

7

Spiracles

How can a buried stingray keep breathing without gulping sandy water through its mouth?

Wikipedia: spiracles just behind the eyes let a ray take in water while buried, so sediment does not flood the mouth path. Soft on efficiency details.

8

Live young

How do stingray babies usually arrive?

Wikipedia: stingrays are ovoviviparous and bear live young. Litter sizes vary; we keep the count soft rather than locking one number.

9

Many kinds

About how many kinds of stingray are there, if we keep the count soft?

Wikipedia puts living stingrays at about 220 known species. Exact totals shift as scientists name or split kinds, so we keep that soft.

10

Myth buster

Do stingrays usually chase and hunt people?

Wikipedia: stingrays are not usually aggressive and ordinarily sting humans only when provoked, such as when stepped on. Fatal stings are very rare. Soft care: shuffle feet in shallow water.
