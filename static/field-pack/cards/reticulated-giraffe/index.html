  • The giraffe is the tallest living land animal.

Score 0/10

1

Tallest

What record does a giraffe hold among living land animals?

The giraffe is the tallest living land animal — taller than any other animal that lives on land today.

2

Food

What do giraffes mostly eat?

Giraffes eat leaves and shoots from tall trees, especially acacia. They are not meat-eaters.

3

Neck

Why does a giraffe have such a long neck?

A giraffe’s extremely long neck helps it reach high foliage that shorter animals cannot.

4

Tongue

What is special about a giraffe’s tongue?

A giraffe uses a very long, dark tongue to pull and strip leaves from thorny branches.

5

Young

What is a baby giraffe called, and what can it do soon after birth?

A baby giraffe is a calf. Newborns can stand soon after they are born.

6

Home

Where do wild giraffes mostly live?

Wild giraffes live on African savannah and open woodland — not only one country.

7

Drink

How does a giraffe drink water?

A giraffe’s neck and legs are so long that it spreads its front legs or bends its knees to reach the water.

8

Ossicones

What are the horn-like bumps on a giraffe’s head?

Those bumps are ossicones — skin-covered, horn-like knobs. They are not antlers that shed.

9

Coat

What is special about a giraffe’s coat?

A reticulated giraffe’s dark patches look like a net. Each giraffe’s spot pattern is unique.

10

Myth buster

Does a giraffe have extra neck bones compared with most mammals?

Like most mammals, a giraffe has seven neck vertebrae. Each bone is just much longer.

Talk about it

  1. Why might a long neck help a giraffe besides reaching high leaves?
  2. If you stood as tall as a giraffe, what would you notice first at the zoo?
  3. Which other animal at the zoo has a special tongue or a long neck?

Push further

  1. A giraffe must spread its front legs to drink. What risk could that pose in the wild?
  2. Each giraffe’s spots are unique. How could that help a keeper tell them apart?
  3. Find one more animal on your zoo map that eats leaves from trees. How does it reach them?
