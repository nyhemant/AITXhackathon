⭐ Sea star

Star-shaped ocean friend — often in touch tanks.

Quick tips (Junior Ranger) — tap to open
  • Star-shaped ocean animals — not fish (better called sea stars).
  • Most have a central disc and about five arms (some kinds have many more).
  • They walk with tiny tube feet on the underside.
  • The mouth is in the middle of the bottom side.
  • Many can regrow a lost arm over time.
1

Not a fish

Are starfish a kind of fish?

Starfish (also called sea stars) are not fish. They lack gills, scales, and fins. They are echinoderms, in the same big group as sea urchins and sea cucumbers. We keep those group names light.

2

Star body

What does a sea star’s body look like?

Wikipedia describes a central disc with radiating arms. About five arms is common, and some kinds grow many more. Exact maxima shift by species.

3

Tube feet

How does a sea star walk?

Sea stars walk with rows of tiny tube feet on the underside. A water-pump system powers those feet so they can move and grip the seafloor. We keep the pump’s denser name for later.

4

Mouth underneath

Where is a sea star’s mouth?

Wikipedia places the mouth on the oral (bottom) side, at the centre of the disc. The top side faces up; the mouth faces the seafloor.

5

Ocean only

Where do sea stars live?

Wikipedia finds starfish in salt water worldwide, from rocky shores to the deep sea. They are marine animals — they do not live in freshwater lakes.

6

Armour

What is a sea star’s skin like?

The upper surface is covered in tough skin with plates and often spines. Colours range from bright orange or red to dull browns and other hues.

7

Predators

What do many sea stars hunt?

Many starfish are predators of slow seafloor animals — clams, snails, and other invertebrates. We keep that feeding story simple and save the stomach-out meal for later.

8

Regrow

What can many sea stars do if they lose an arm?

Many starfish can regenerate a damaged or lost arm. In a few kinds, more of the body can rebuild if part of the central disc remains. — we do not lock a “from one arm” rule.

9

Many kinds

How many kinds of sea star are there?

Wikipedia puts living starfish at about two thousand known kinds worldwide. Exact totals shift as scientists name or split kinds.

10

Myth buster

Does the name “starfish” mean they are fish?

Wikipedia prefers sea star because they are not fish. They are invertebrates — animals without a backbone — even though the older name “starfish” sounds like a fish.

Facts from Wikipedia, Starfish. Where sources disagree on exact numbers, we keep them approximate.
