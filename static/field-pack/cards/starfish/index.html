⭐ Sea star

Star-shaped ocean friend — often in touch tanks.

1

Not a fish

Are starfish a kind of fish?

Wikipedia says starfish (also called sea stars) are not fish. They lack gills, scales, and fins. They are echinoderms, in the same big group as sea urchins and sea cucumbers. We keep those group names light.

2

Star body

What does a sea star’s body look like, if we keep the arm count soft?

Wikipedia describes a central disc with radiating arms. About five arms is common, and some kinds grow many more. Exact maxima shift by species, so we keep that count soft.

3

Tube feet

How does a sea star walk?

Wikipedia says sea stars walk with rows of tiny tube feet on the underside. A water-pump system powers those feet so they can move and grip the seafloor. We keep the pump’s denser name for later.

4

Mouth underneath

Where is a sea star’s mouth?

Wikipedia places the mouth on the oral (bottom) side, at the centre of the disc. The top side faces up; the mouth faces the seafloor.

5

Ocean only

Where do sea stars live?

Wikipedia finds starfish in salt water worldwide, from rocky shores to the deep sea. They are marine animals — they do not live in freshwater lakes.

6

Armour

What is a sea star’s skin like?

Wikipedia says the upper surface is covered in tough skin with plates and often spines. Colours range from bright orange or red to dull browns and other hues. We keep plate names soft.

7

Predators

What do many sea stars hunt?

Wikipedia says many starfish are predators of slow seafloor animals — clams, snails, and other invertebrates. We keep that feeding story simple and save the stomach-out meal for later.

8

Regrow

What can many sea stars do if they lose an arm?

Wikipedia says many starfish can regenerate a damaged or lost arm. In a few kinds, more of the body can rebuild if part of the central disc remains. Edge cases stay soft — we do not lock a “from one arm” rule.

9

Many kinds

How many kinds of sea star are there, if we keep the count soft?

Wikipedia puts living starfish at about two thousand known kinds worldwide. Exact totals shift as scientists name or split kinds, so we keep that count soft.

10

Myth buster

Does the name “starfish” mean they are fish?

Wikipedia prefers sea star because they are not fish. They are invertebrates — animals without a backbone — even though the older name “starfish” sounds like a fish.

Talk about it

  1. Tiny tube feet run on a hidden water-pump. Why might canals of fluid beat a red-blood heart?
  2. Some sea stars push a stomach out to eat a clam. Why might that messy meal work?
  3. Taking away a few sea stars can change a whole rocky pool. Why might one hunter matter so much?
