1

Smallest otter

What kind of otter is the Asian small-clawed otter?

Wikipedia says the Asian small-clawed otter is the smallest otter species — not the biggest, and not a heavy sea otter.

2

Wet Asia home

Where do Asian small-clawed otters live in the wild?

Wikipedia places them in riverine habitats, freshwater wetlands, and mangrove swamps in South and Southeast Asia. They also use rice paddies. They are not ice or open-ocean-only animals.

3

Crab snacks

What do Asian small-clawed otters often eat?

Wikipedia says they feed on crabs, molluscs, and other small aquatic animals. They often feel for those snacks with their paws.

4

Short claws

What is special about their claws?

Wikipedia says they have short claws that do not extend beyond the pads of their webbed digits. Those handy paws help them hunt in mud.

5

Family groups

How do Asian small-clawed otters usually live?

Wikipedia says they live in pairs and also in family groups. They are not always alone, and they are not huge flocks.

6

Chatty squeaks

What sounds do they make?

Wikipedia says group members communicate with many distinct calls and utter a variety of yelps and other chatty sounds. Exact call counts stay soft.

7

Pup

What is a baby otter called?

Wikipedia calls a baby otter a pup. A joey is a baby kangaroo, and a calf is a baby cow or whale.

8

Thick fur

How do they stay warm in the water?

Otters have thick, waterproof fur. That dense coat helps them stay warm in water — they are not scaly fish.

9

Webbed swimmers

How do their feet help them move?

Wikipedia notes their webbed digits. Webbed feet help them swim. They do not have wings, and they do swim.

10

Myth buster

Is an otter the same animal as a beaver?

Wikipedia treats otters as meat-eating weasel-family animals. Beavers are rodents that eat plants and chew wood. They are not the same animal, and neither is a fish.
