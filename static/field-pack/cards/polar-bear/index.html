1

Arctic home

Where do polar bears live in the wild?

Wikipedia says polar bears are native to the Arctic and nearby areas. They live on sea ice and nearby land. They are not Antarctic animals.

2

White-looking fur

Why does polar bear fur look white?

Wikipedia says the fur looks white or yellowish. The long guard hairs are see-through and scatter light, so the coat looks white.

3

Black skin

What color is the skin under a polar bear’s fur?

Wikipedia says polar bear skin is black. The nose and foot pads show that dark skin under the white-looking fur.

4

Ice-and-paddle paws

How do a polar bear’s huge paws help?

Wikipedia says the large paws let the bear walk on ice and paddle in the water. Big paws spread weight on ice.

5

Seal snacks

What do polar bears specialize in hunting?

Wikipedia says polar bears specialize on seals, especially ringed seals, and hunt them from the sea ice.

6

Strong swimmers

How do polar bears swim?

Wikipedia says polar bears paddle in the water with their large paws. They are strong swimmers.

7

Fat jacket

How does a polar bear stay warm in the cold?

Wikipedia says polar bears have a thick layer of fat under the skin that helps keep them warm.

8

Cub dens

Where do polar bear moms have their cubs?

Wikipedia says mothers give birth to cubs in winter dens. Cubs stay with their mother a long time. We do not lock the exact years.

9

Soft ice care

Why does healthy sea ice matter for polar bears?

Wikipedia says polar bears hunt seals from the sea ice. Healthy ice helps them get to those hunting spots. We stay with that soft care — no status letter.

10

Myth buster

Do polar bears live at the South Pole?

Wikipedia says polar bears live in the Arctic. Penguins live in Antarctica. Wild polar bears and penguins do not meet.
