1

Reef home

Where do clownfish usually live?

Wikipedia says clownfish live on coral reefs in warm Indo-Pacific waters. Their homes are shallow reefs and nearby lagoon water.

2

Orange stripes

What do many clownfish look like?

Wikipedia says clownfish often have white bars on a red, orange, yellow, brown, or black body. Many look bright orange with white bands; some kinds look darker.

3

Anemone partner

Where do clownfish find shelter?

Wikipedia says clownfish live with sea anemones. They use the anemone’s tentacles for shelter and protection from predators.

4

Safe mucus

Why can a clownfish sit in stinging tentacles?

Wikipedia says a mucus coating on the skin helps clownfish avoid being stung. How that mucus is made stays a later story.

5

Tentacle hideout

What does a clownfish do when danger comes?

Wikipedia says a clownfish that wanders out hurries back to the tentacles when it senses a threat. The anemone is its safe hideout.

6

Family group

How do clownfish usually live around an anemone?

Wikipedia says clownfish live in a small group with a breeding pair and some younger fish. They share one anemone home.

7

Egg care

Where do clownfish put their eggs, and who helps?

Wikipedia says the female lays eggs on a rock near the anemone. A parent helps clean, fan, and guard them.

8

Indo-Pacific home

Where in the world do wild clownfish live?

Wikipedia says clownfish live in warm tropical waters of the Indian Ocean and the western Pacific.

9

Soft care

How can people help wild clownfish?

Wikipedia ties clownfish to healthy reef anemones. Soft care is protecting reefs and anemone homes — we do not lock one status letter.

10

Many kinds

Is there only one “Nemo” kind of clownfish?

Wikipedia lists many kinds of clownfish, also called anemonefish. The familiar movie fish is just one face, not the only kind.
