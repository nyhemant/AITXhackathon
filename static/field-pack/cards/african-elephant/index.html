Talk

Learn first — tap to open
  • The African elephant is the largest living land animal.
  • It eats plants — grass, leaves, and bark — not meat.
  • Its trunk can grab, drink, spray, and smell.
  • A baby elephant is a calf.
  • A herd is led by the oldest female.

Score 0/10

1

Biggest

What record does an African elephant hold among living land animals?

The African elephant is the largest and heaviest living land animal.

2

Food

What do African elephants eat?

African elephants are herbivores. They eat plants such as grass, leaves, and bark — not meat.

3

Trunk

What does an elephant use its trunk for?

The trunk is a long nose and upper lip. Elephants use it to grab food, drink, spray water, and smell.

4

Ears

Why does an African elephant have such big ears?

Big ears help an African elephant shed heat and stay cool.

5

Young

What is a baby elephant called?

A baby elephant is a calf.

6

Family

Who usually leads an elephant herd?

Elephant families live in a herd led by the oldest female, called the matriarch.

7

Tusks

What are an elephant’s tusks?

Tusks are very long teeth — not horns.

8

Voice

What sound is an elephant famous for?

An elephant’s famous call is a trumpet — a loud sound made through the trunk.

9

Water

How can an African elephant swim?

Elephants can swim. They sometimes swim underwater and use the trunk like a snorkel.

10

Myth buster

Can an elephant jump?

Elephants cannot jump. Their heavy bodies stay on the ground.
