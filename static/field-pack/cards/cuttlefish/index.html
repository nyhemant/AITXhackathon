🦑 Cuttlefish

Color-changing cousin of the octopus — a living disguise.

PhotosLearn more

  • Marine molluscs related to squid and octopus
  • Unique internal shell (cuttlebone) for buoyancy
  • Eight arms plus two longer hunting tentacles
  • Change skin color and pattern very fast
  • Squirt ink to hide or confuse predators

Score 0/10

1

Cephalopod

What kind of animal is a cuttlefish?

Wikipedia says cuttlefish are marine molluscs in the same big group as squid, octopuses, and nautiluses — cephalopods.

2

Cuttlebone

What does the cuttlebone help a cuttlefish do?

Wikipedia says the cuttlebone is a unique porous internal shell. Cuttlefish change the gas-to-liquid mix inside it to float up or sink.

3

Arms and tentacles

How many arms and tentacles does a cuttlefish have?

Wikipedia says cuttlefish have eight arms and two longer tentacles with suckers, which they use to grab prey.

4

Camouflage

Why are cuttlefish nicknamed “chameleons of the sea”?

Wikipedia says cuttlefish are sometimes called “chameleons of the sea” because they can change skin color and pattern in less than a second.

5

Ink

How can a cuttlefish use ink when a predator comes?

Wikipedia says cuttlefish can squirt dark ink as a smoke-screen cloud, or release a look-alike decoy, then swim away.

6

W eyes

What do cuttlefish eyes look like in bright light?

Wikipedia says cuttlefish have large, W-shaped pupils. The W shows clearly in bright light.

7

Hunt

What do cuttlefish eat?

Wikipedia says cuttlefish eat crabs, shrimp, fish, and sometimes other cuttlefish. Small molluscs can be snacks too.

8

Short life

About how long do cuttlefish usually live?

Wikipedia says a typical cuttlefish life is about one to two years. Exact ages can differ by kind, so we keep that soft.

9

Soft care

How can people help cuttlefish?

Wikipedia ties cuttlefish to mostly shallow coastal waters where they hunt and lay eggs. Soft care is keeping those waters healthy — we do not lock one status letter.

10

Myth buster

Are cuttlefish a kind of fish?

Wikipedia says cuttlefish are marine molluscs, not fish. They have soft bodies and an internal shell, and they sit with squid and octopus, not with tuna.
