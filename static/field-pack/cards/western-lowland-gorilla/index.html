Learn first — tap to open
  • Wild western lowland gorillas live in Central African forests — rainforest and swamp forest.
  • They eat mostly plants — fruit, leaves, stems, and bark — and sometimes insects.
  • A silverback is an adult male with a silver-grey back who leads the family.
  • A family troop is females and young living with the silverback.
  • They are the smallest kind of gorilla — and still huge.

Score 0/10

1

Central Africa forests

Where do wild western lowland gorillas live?

Wikipedia says they live in forest and lowland swampland in central Africa — rainforest and swamp forest, not ice or desert.

2

Mostly plants

What do western lowland gorillas mostly eat?

Wikipedia says they are primarily herbivorous — fruit, leaves, stems, and bark — and may eat insects from time to time.

3

Silverback

What is a silverback?

Wikipedia says older males get grey hair on the back and rump — that is why they are called silverbacks — and the silverback leads the group.

4

Family troop

Who usually lives with a silverback?

Wikipedia says breeding groups are one silverback, adult females, and their offspring. The male’s role is protector.

5

Smallest gorilla

How does a western lowland gorilla’s size compare with other gorillas?

Wikipedia calls it the smallest of the four gorilla subspecies. We do not lock one weight.

6

Knuckle-walk

How do western lowland gorillas usually walk on the ground?

Wikipedia says they walk in a hunched, four-legged way with hands curled and knuckles touching the ground.

7

Display, not mean

What does a chest-beat or charge usually mean?

Wikipedia says a silverback may stand up, charge, and pound his chest, but gorillas are generally calm and nonaggressive unless they are disturbed.

8

Baby rides

How do baby western lowland gorillas travel with mom?

Wikipedia says infants cling to mom’s fur and ride on her back for years — not just a day.

9

Night nests

Where do western lowland gorillas sleep at night?

Wikipedia notes night nests — some in trees, many on the ground. They build leafy nests for the night.

10

Myth buster — King Kong

Are western lowland gorillas movie monsters like King Kong?

Wikipedia describes plant-eating family groups led by a silverback — not movie monsters.
