Where do wild giant pandas live?

Wikipedia says the giant panda lives in mountain forests in China. It is not an African or Antarctic animal.

What kind of animal is a giant panda?

Wikipedia says the giant panda is a bear. People once mixed it up with raccoons, but it is a true bear.

What does a giant panda’s coat look like?

Wikipedia says the giant panda has a white coat with black patches around the eyes, ears, legs, and shoulders.

How do giant pandas spend much of the day?

Wikipedia says bamboo is low in nutrition, so giant pandas need to eat often. They spend much of the day feeding. We do not lock exact hours.

What is a newborn giant panda cub like?

Wikipedia says a newborn cub is pink, blind, and toothless. It is tiny at birth. The famous black-and-white look comes later. We do not lock exact weights.

How good are giant pandas at climbing trees?

Wikipedia says giant pandas can climb and take shelter in trees. Young pandas are often especially good climbers.

Giant grass

What kind of plant is bamboo?

Bamboo is a kind of giant grass, not a tree. Giant pandas eat this grass almost all the time.

How does an extra bump on a giant panda’s front paw help?

Wikipedia says a thumb-like bump on the front paw helps hold bamboo in place for eating. How that bone is built in more detail comes later.
