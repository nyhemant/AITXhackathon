Quick tips (Junior Ranger) — tap to open
  • Black-and-white seabird with a big colorful bill
  • Often nicknamed “clown of the sea” or “sea parrot”
  • Nest in burrows on rocky islands and cliffs
  • Can carry many small fish crosswise in one bill
  • Spend most of the year out at sea
1

North Atlantic home

Where do Atlantic puffins spend their year?

Wikipedia says Atlantic puffins breed on cold northern coasts and islands. They spend autumn and winter out on the open ocean.

2

Colorful bill

What does an Atlantic puffin’s bill look like in breeding season?

Wikipedia says the breeding-season bill is boldly marked with orange-red, yellow, and darker plates. That bright color shows in spring.

3

Burrow nest

Where does an Atlantic puffin usually put its nest?

Wikipedia says puffins nest in clifftop colonies. They dig a burrow, and they may also use a crevice among rocks.

4

One egg

How many chicks do Atlantic puffins usually raise in a season?

Wikipedia says the female usually lays a single white egg each year. That one chick is the season’s family.

5

Fish in the bill

How can an Atlantic puffin carry food home?

Wikipedia says a puffin can catch several small fish in one dive. A grooved tongue and a special bill hold them in a crosswise row.

6

Underwater wings

How does an Atlantic puffin swim after fish?

Wikipedia says puffins swim underwater using their wings like paddles. Their feet work as a rudder to steer.

7

Land waddle

How do Atlantic puffins often walk on land?

Wikipedia notes the puffin’s waddle on land. Short legs set far back give that upright, side-to-side walk.

8

Soft care

How can people help wild puffins?

Wikipedia ties puffins to predator-free nesting islands and to fish in the sea. Soft care is healthy islands plus healthy oceans — no status letter.

9

Colony life

How do Atlantic puffins usually nest?

Wikipedia says puffins are sociable and usually breed in large clifftop colonies. Neighbors nest close together.

10

Not a penguin

Is an Atlantic puffin a penguin?

Wikipedia says Atlantic puffins fly in the air over the sea. Penguins are different birds that do not fly in the air.

Facts from Wikipedia, Atlantic puffin. Where sources disagree on exact numbers, we keep them approximate.
