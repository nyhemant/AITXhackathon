1

Australia home

Where do wild koalas live?

Wikipedia says wild koalas live in eucalyptus forests of eastern and southeastern Australia. They are not Antarctic or African animals.

2

Leaf specialist

What do koalas almost always eat?

Wikipedia says eucalyptus leaves make up most of a koala’s diet. Those leaves are tough, low-energy, and toxic to many other animals.

3

Tree life

Where does a koala spend almost all of its time?

Wikipedia says the koala is a tree animal. It spends almost all its time up in trees and usually comes down only to move to another tree.

4

Sleepy saver

Why do koalas sleep most of the day?

Wikipedia says eucalyptus leaves give little energy. Koalas rest most of the day to save that energy. We do not lock one exact sleep number.

5

Joey

What is a baby koala called, and where does it keep growing?

Wikipedia says a baby koala is a joey. Like other marsupials, it crawls into mom’s pouch and keeps growing there.

6

Climb grip

How do a koala’s front paws help it climb?

Wikipedia says the front paws have two opposable digits — like two thumbs — opposite three fingers. That grip helps a koala hold branches.

7

Fluffy look

What makes the classic koala face?

Wikipedia says a koala is easy to know by its large head, round fluffy ears, and big dark nose.

8

Mostly alone

How do adult koalas usually live?

Wikipedia says adult koalas are mostly solitary. The main family bond is a mother and her joey.

9

Leaf water

How do koalas usually get the water they need?

Wikipedia says a koala can get enough water from its leaves, so it does not need to drink often.

10

Myth buster — koala bear

People sometimes say “koala bear.” Is a koala a bear?

Wikipedia says the koala is sometimes inaccurately called a koala bear. It is a marsupial with a pouch, not a bear.
