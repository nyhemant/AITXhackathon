Quick tips (Junior Ranger) — tap to open
  • Not a bear — a marsupial with a pouch.
  • Lives in eucalyptus forests in Australia.
  • Eats eucalyptus leaves.
  • A baby is a joey (grows in mom’s pouch).
  • Sleeps most of the day to save energy.
1

Australia home

Where do wild koalas live?

Wild koalas live in eucalyptus forests of eastern and southeastern Australia. They are not Antarctic or African animals.

2

Leaf specialist

What do koalas almost always eat?

Eucalyptus leaves make up most of a koala’s diet. Those leaves are tough, low-energy, and toxic to many other animals.

3

Tree life

Where does a koala spend almost all of its time?

The koala is a tree animal. It spends almost all its time up in trees and usually comes down only to move to another tree.

4

Sleepy saver

Why do koalas sleep most of the day?

Eucalyptus leaves give little energy. Koalas rest most of the day to save that energy. We do not lock one exact sleep number.

5

Joey

What is a baby koala called, and where does it keep growing?

A baby koala is a joey. Like other marsupials, it crawls into mom’s pouch and keeps growing there.

6

Climb grip

How do a koala’s front paws help it climb?

The front paws have two opposable digits — like two thumbs — opposite three fingers. That grip helps a koala hold branches.

7

Fluffy look

What makes the classic koala face?

A koala is easy to know by its large head, round fluffy ears, and big dark nose.

8

Mostly alone

How do adult koalas usually live?

Adult koalas are mostly solitary. The main family bond is a mother and her joey.

9

Leaf water

How do koalas usually get the water they need?

A koala can get enough water from its leaves, so it does not need to drink often.

10

Myth buster — koala bear

People sometimes say “koala bear.” Is a koala a bear?

The koala is sometimes inaccurately called a koala bear. It is a marsupial with a pouch, not a bear.

Facts from Wikipedia, Koala. Where sources disagree on exact numbers, we keep them approximate.
