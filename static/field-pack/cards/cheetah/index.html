Score 0/10

1

Fastest on land

What is a cheetah famous for?

Wikipedia calls the cheetah the fastest land animal. We do not lock one exact speed.

2

Solid spots

What kind of marks does a cheetah’s coat have?

Wikipedia says a cheetah’s fur is marked with evenly spaced, solid black spots. A leopard has rosettes instead.

3

Tear marks

What are the black lines on a cheetah’s face?

Wikipedia describes black tear-like facial streaks from the corners of the eyes down the side of the nose.

4

No roar

What sounds is a cheetah known for?

Wikipedia lists bird-like chirps and a loud purr. Cheetahs are not known for a lion-like roar.

5

Savannah home

Where do most wild cheetahs live?

Wikipedia says cheetahs in eastern and southern Africa occur mostly in savannas. They seldom live in tropical forests.

6

Sprint body

How is a cheetah’s body built for a chase?

Wikipedia says the cheetah evolved for speed, with powerful hindlimb muscles, long limbs, and a flexible spine. Its slim form is built for running.

7

Steering tail

How does a cheetah’s long tail help in a chase?

Wikipedia says the long tail works like a rudder. It helps the cheetah turn and stay balanced while chasing prey.

8

Meat hunter

What do wild cheetahs mostly hunt?

Wikipedia says cheetahs prefer medium-sized hooved animals such as impala, springbok, and Thomson’s gazelles.

9

Cub cape

What special fur do cheetah cubs have on their backs?

Wikipedia says a cub’s nape, shoulders, and back are thickly covered with long bluish-grey hair called a mantle. That fluffy cape can help the cub stay hidden.

10

Myth buster — marathon

Do cheetahs run fast for a long time, like a marathon?

Wikipedia describes short, high-speed chases — often under a minute — not long marathon runs. After a hunt, a cheetah needs to rest.

Talk about it

  1. Cheetahs hunt in the day, while lions often hunt at night. How could that help them share the plains?
  2. Lions and hyenas often steal a cheetah’s meal. How could a cheetah still get enough to eat?
  3. A cheetah sneaks close before it sprints. Why not start the chase from far away?

Push further

  1. Cheetah claws stay out more than a house cat’s. What would you ask a keeper about that?
  2. Some brother cheetahs hunt together. Why might a team help on the plains?
  3. Wild cheetahs can look very alike. What would you ask a keeper about telling them apart?
