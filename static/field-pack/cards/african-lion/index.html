1

Family

What do you call a group of lions?

Lions live together in a pride — a social group that shares space and raises cubs.

2

Food

What do lions mostly eat?

Lions eat meat. They mostly hunt hooved animals such as zebra, wildebeest, and antelope.

3

Voice

What sound is a lion famous for?

A lion’s roar can travel a long way — about 8 km / 5 miles (Wikipedia).

4

Mane

Which lion usually grows a big fluffy mane?

Male lions often grow a big mane around the head and neck.

5

Young

What is a baby lion called?

A baby lion is a cub. Many cubs have faint spots that fade as they grow.

6

Home

Where do wild lions mostly live?

Wild lions mostly live on grassland, savannah, and shrubland — rarely in closed forest.

7

Rest

How much of the day do lions often spend resting?

Lions often rest or stay inactive for about 20 hours a day.

8

Hunting

In a pride, who usually does most of the hunting?

In a pride, lionesses usually do most of the hunting, often together.

9

Tail

What special tip does a lion’s tail have?

A lion’s tail ends in a dark hairy tuft.

10

Myth buster

Do lions really live in a jungle like in some cartoons?

Cartoons show jungle lions, but wild lions mostly live on open grassland.

Facts from Wikipedia, Lion.
