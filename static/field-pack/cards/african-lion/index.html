Talk

1

Mane

Does this lion have a big fluffy mane?

Grown-up males often have a mane. Females and cubs usually don’t.

2

Look close

What do you notice?

Cubs can still show faint spots. Grown-ups usually don’t.

3

Pride

Lions often live in a group called a pride. How many do you see?

A pride is a family group. Some days you only see one resting.

4

Food

Meat eater or plant eater?

Lions eat meat. They are hunters, not grazers.

5

See & say

Did we see a lion — and what would you tell a grown-up?

Look, then say one true thing.

More talk

Why is a lion’s roar so loud?
So other lions far away can hear it — to call the pride and warn “this is our place.”
