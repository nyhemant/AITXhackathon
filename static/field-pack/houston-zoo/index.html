More talk

Why is a giraffe’s neck so long? How does it drink?
A long neck reaches high leaves. To drink, a giraffe spreads its front legs and bends down carefully.

McGovern gorillas — who looks calmest?

More talk

Why are gorillas so strong? Do they live in families?
Strong arms help them climb and pull plants. They live in family groups led by a silverback.

🦁 African lion

Houston lion pride — listen for a roar, then wait.

More talk

Why does a lion have a mane? Why do they live in a pride?
A mane makes a lion look bigger and protects the neck. A pride is a family that hunts and raises cubs together.

Lion cam at the Smithsonian National Zoo · African lions · Open Virtual Field Trip

🐯 Tiger

Tiger water’s edge — why put a pool in the habitat?

More talk

Why does a tiger have stripes? Are they like a house cat?
Stripes break up their shape in grass and shade. Yes — tigers are big cats in the same family as house cats.

Houston flamingo flock — pink on the water’s edge.

More talk

Why do flamingos have long legs? Why stand on one?
Long legs let them wade and find food. Standing on one leg rests the other and can help them stay warm.
