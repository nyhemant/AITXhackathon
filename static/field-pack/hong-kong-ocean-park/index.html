Planning a kid-friendly day at Ocean Park Hong Kong in Hong Kong? Field Trip Kit by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of animals so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

Instead of racing through every exhibit, start with top picks like Giant panda, Asian small-clawed otter, Red panda, Sea turtle, Shark, and Jellyfish. That keeps toddlers and school-age kids engaged without overwhelm.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck or trunk. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Weather or crowds in Hong Kong can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.

  4. Find a long neck or trunk
  5. Hear a loud sound (or a quiet one)
  6. Point to where an animal might sleep
  7. Find a bird that loves water
