Planning a kid-friendly day at The DoSeum in San Antonio, Texas? Field Trip Kit by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of play zones so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

The shortlist highlights Outdoor Area, Imaginarium, Woven Wonders, Creative Makery, Art Lab, and Waterfall play. Each pick has a kid-sized blurb so a preschooler or early elementary explorer can understand why it is cool before you arrive.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Climb something colorful; Make or build with your hands; Find water play (if open); Try a quiet looking spot. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Practical tip for San Antonio: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.

About this place: Play-and-learn museum day. Everything here is free to print and share with co-parents or grandparents joining the day.

Treasure hunt checklist (printable)

Sample finds from the free one-page hunt for The DoSeum:

  4. Try a quiet looking spot
