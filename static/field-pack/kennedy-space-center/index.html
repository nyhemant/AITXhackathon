Planning a kid-friendly day at Kennedy Space Center in Merritt Island, Florida? Baby's Day Out by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of stops so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

Your printable kit focuses on Rockets & launch, Space shuttle / orbiter, Astronaut life, Planet / space hall, Hands-on lab, and Outdoor Area — a manageable set for Pre-K through about grade 5, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find a rocket taller than a house; Spot an astronaut suit; See a spacecraft up close; Find a countdown or launch clue. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Weather or crowds in FL can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.
