Planning a kid-friendly day at Kennedy Space Center in Merritt Island, Florida? Field Trip Kit by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of stops so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

Your printable kit focuses on Space shuttle / orbiter, Rockets & launch, Astronaut life, Planet / space hall, Hands-on lab, and Free explore — a manageable set for young kids through about age 10–11, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find a rocket taller than a house; Spot an astronaut suit; See a spacecraft up close; Find a countdown or launch clue. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.
