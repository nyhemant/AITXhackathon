Heading to Kennedy Space Center in Merritt Island, Florida with kids? Here’s a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of stops — so you’re not trying to do the whole place.

Focus on Space shuttle / orbiter, Rockets & launch, Astronaut life, Planet / space hall, Hands-on lab, and Free explore. That set usually works from preschool through about age 10, with room to cut stops if legs get tired.

The hunt is one page with checkboxes. Examples: Find a rocket taller than a house; Spot an astronaut suit; See a spacecraft up close; Find a countdown or launch clue. No scores — print at home (or hotel) and bring a pen.

If weather or crowds in FL shift the plan, reorder the list — nothing here requires a fixed path.
