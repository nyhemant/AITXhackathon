Pick a few animals, print a one-page checklist to carry, and have something to talk about on the way. Free, no signup.

South Rim village — rail overlooks and a short rim walk, not the whole canyon

  • Timehalf day
  • SettingAltitude + sun; stay behind rails; little kids tire fast.
  • TicketsPark entrance pass; South Rim is the main family slice.
  • Get thereVillage parking fills early; free shuttle on the rim in season.
