🦁 Dallas Zoo is a classic zoo stop in Dallas, Texas. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

Your printable kit focuses on African elephant, Reticulated giraffe, African lion, Sumatran tiger, Western lowland gorilla, and African penguin — a manageable set for Pre-K through about grade 5, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck, trunk, or horns. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

For Dallas Zoo, arrive with the one-page hunt in your bag, pick a first target from the shortlist, and treat the rest as bonus — a calm day beats a complete checklist.

Treasure hunt checklist (printable)

Sample finds from the free one-page hunt for Dallas Zoo:

  1. Find an animal taller than a grown-up
  2. Spot stripes or spots
  3. Watch something swim or splash
  4. Find a long neck, trunk, or horns
  5. Hear a loud sound (or a quiet one)
  6. Point to where an animal might sleep
  7. Find a bird with bright feathers
  8. Photo your favorite animal
