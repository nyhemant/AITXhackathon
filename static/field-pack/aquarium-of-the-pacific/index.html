Visiting Aquarium of the Pacific with kids does not have to mean wandering until everyone is tired. This free one-page scavenger hunt for Long Beach, California keeps the day focused and fun.

Instead of racing through every exhibit, start with top picks like Shark, Stingray, Seahorse, Jellyfish, Clownfish, and Sea turtle. That keeps toddlers and school-age kids engaged without overwhelm.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find something with fins; Spot a flat swimmer (like a ray); Find a shell or hard cover; Watch bubbles or moving water. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.
