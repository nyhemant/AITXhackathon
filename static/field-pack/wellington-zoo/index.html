Planning a kid-friendly day at Wellington Zoo in Wellington, New Zealand? Field Trip Kit by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of animals so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

The shortlist highlights African lion, Sumatran tiger, Reticulated giraffe, African elephant, Western lowland gorilla, and African penguin. Each pick has a kid-sized blurb so a preschooler or early elementary explorer can understand why it is cool before you arrive.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck or trunk. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Practical tip for Wellington: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.

Treasure hunt checklist (printable)

Sample finds from the free one-page hunt for Wellington Zoo:

  1. Find an animal taller than a grown-up
  2. Spot stripes or spots
  3. Watch something swim or splash
  4. Find a long neck or trunk
  5. Hear a loud sound (or a quiet one)
  6. Point to where an animal might sleep
  7. Find a bird that loves water
  8. Photo your favorite animal
