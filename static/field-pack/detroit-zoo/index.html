🐧 Penguin

Penguin Conservation Center — waddle, dive, kid magnet.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet, Cold / snow
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why can’t penguins fly? Why the black-and-white coat?
Their wings became flippers for swimming. Dark backs and white bellies help them hide from above and below.

🦒 Giraffe

Tall necks above the tree line — easy wow.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Plants / leaves
Where is home?
Grassland
What is their superpower?
Run fast, Stomp / strong
Why is a giraffe’s neck so long? How does it drink?
A long neck reaches high leaves. To drink, a giraffe spreads its front legs and bends down carefully.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Plants / leaves
Where is home?
Forest
What is their superpower?
Climb, Stomp / strong
Why are gorillas so strong? Do they live in families?
Strong arms help them climb and pull plants. They live in family groups led by a silverback.

🦁 African lion

Pride of the grasslands — look for cubs when on view.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat
Where is home?
Grassland
What is their superpower?
Run fast, Stomp / strong, Hide
Why does a lion have a mane? Why do they live in a pride?
A mane makes a lion look bigger and protects the neck. A pride is a family that hunts and raises cubs together.

🐯 Tiger

Amur tiger — stripes and stealth.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat
Where is home?
Forest
What is their superpower?
Run fast, Hide, Stomp / strong, Swim
Why does a tiger have stripes? Are they like a house cat?
Stripes break up their shape in grass and shade. Yes — tigers are big cats in the same family as house cats.
