Planning a kid-friendly day at Woodland Park Zoo in Seattle, WA? Field Trip Kit by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of animals so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

Your printable kit focuses on Western lowland gorilla, African elephant, Reticulated giraffe, Sumatran tiger, Ring-tailed lemur, and African lion — a manageable set for young kids through about age 10–11, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck, trunk, or horns. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Weather or crowds in WA can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.

Rain-friendly indoor pockets plus big outdoor habitats. Field Trip Kit is free to use. Open the full interactive list when you want optional Q&A cards, or use the one-page hunt alone for a lighter outing.
