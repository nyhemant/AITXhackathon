Planning a kid-friendly day at Carnegie Museum of Natural History in Pittsburgh, PA? Baby's Day Out by 1Less gives you a free printable scavenger hunt and a short list of stops so you are not staring at a map of hundreds of options.

Instead of racing through every exhibit, start with top picks like Dinosaur hall, Animal / mammal hall, Planet / space hall, Hands-on lab, Imaginarium, and Free explore. That keeps toddlers and school-age kids engaged without overwhelm.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find something taller than you; Spot a model or artifact; Try one hands-on activity; Read a kid label out loud. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Weather or crowds in PA can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.

About this place: Dinos in Pittsburgh. Everything here is free to print and share with co-parents or grandparents joining the day.
