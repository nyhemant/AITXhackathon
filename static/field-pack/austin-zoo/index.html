Visiting Austin Zoo with kids does not have to mean wandering until everyone is tired. This free one-page scavenger hunt for Austin, Texas keeps the day focused and fun.

The shortlist highlights Reticulated giraffe, African lion, Sumatran tiger, Two-toed sloth, Ring-tailed lemur, and Galápagos tortoise. Each pick has a kid-sized blurb so a preschooler or early elementary explorer can understand why it is cool before you arrive.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck, trunk, or horns. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

For Austin Zoo, arrive with the one-page hunt in your bag, pick a first target from the shortlist, and treat the rest as bonus — a calm day beats a complete checklist.

Treasure hunt checklist (printable)

Sample finds from the free one-page hunt for Austin Zoo:

  1. Find an animal taller than a grown-up
  2. Spot stripes or spots
  3. Watch something swim or splash
  4. Find a long neck, trunk, or horns
  5. Hear a loud sound (or a quiet one)
  6. Point to where an animal might sleep
  7. Find a bird with bright feathers
  8. Photo your favorite animal
