Visiting Museum of Science with kids does not have to mean wandering until everyone is tired. This free one-page scavenger hunt for Boston, MA keeps the day focused and fun.

Your printable kit focuses on Hands-on lab, Planet / space hall, Dinosaur hall, Creative Makery, Imaginarium, and Free explore — a manageable set for young kids through about age 10–11, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find something taller than you; Spot a model or artifact; Try one hands-on activity; Read a kid label out loud. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Practical tip for Boston: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.
