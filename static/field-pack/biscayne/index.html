Animals you might meet

Mostly from a boat or the jetty — not a promise. Give wildlife lots of space — look, don’t touch, and stay far back.

🐢 Sea turtle

Sometimes in the bay or on the reef — usually from a boat. Never promised.

🦈 Shark

Coastal and reef waters — usually from a boat. Sightings aren’t promised.

🐟 Stingray

Shallow sand and seagrass — shuffle, give space, and never promised.

🐙 Octopus

Caribbean reef octopus hide on the reef — usually from a boat. Never promised.

🌊 Seahorse

Tiny seahorses live in seagrass and shallows — hard to spot and never promised.

🦀 Crab

Shore, mangroves, and shallows — look, don’t chase.

⭐ Sea star

Reef sea stars — usually from a boat. Look, don’t touch.
