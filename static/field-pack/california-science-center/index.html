🛰️ California Science Center is a classic science museum stop in Los Angeles, California. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

Your printable kit focuses on Space shuttle / orbiter, Rockets & launch, Hands-on lab, Planet / space hall, Aquarium zone, and Rainforest dome — a manageable set for young kids through about age 10–11, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find something taller than you; Spot a model, bone, or real artifact; Try one hands-on activity; Read a kid label out loud. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Practical tip for Los Angeles: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.

Endeavour when timed tickets allow; otherwise ecosystems and hands-on halls. 1Less keeps the promise simple: one less decision before a museum or zoo day with kids.
