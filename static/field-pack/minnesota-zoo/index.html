Visiting Minnesota Zoo with kids does not have to mean wandering until everyone is tired. This free one-page scavenger hunt for Apple Valley, MN keeps the day focused and fun.

Instead of racing through every exhibit, start with top picks like African elephant, Reticulated giraffe, African lion, Sumatran tiger, Western lowland gorilla, and African penguin. That keeps toddlers and school-age kids engaged without overwhelm.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck, trunk, or horns. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Weather or crowds in MN can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.

About this place: Twin Cities zoo day shortlist. Everything here is free to print and share with co-parents or grandparents joining the day.

Treasure hunt checklist (printable)

Sample finds from the free one-page hunt for Minnesota Zoo:

  1. Find an animal taller than a grown-up
  2. Spot stripes or spots
  3. Watch something swim or splash
  4. Find a long neck, trunk, or horns
  5. Hear a loud or quiet animal sound
  6. Point to a sleep spot
  7. Find bright feathers
  8. Photo your favorite animal
