🏔️ Banff National Park

Banff — Lake Louise shoreline and townsite views; not the whole Rockies

  • Timehalf day
  • SettingAltitude mild; cold water; crowds summer/fall.
  • TicketsParks Canada discovery pass or day fee; parking fills early at Louise.
  • Get thereBanff town + shuttle options to Lake Louise in peak season.
