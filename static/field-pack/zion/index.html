🏜️ Zion National Park

Zion Canyon shuttle corridor — red walls and riverside paths, not the full Narrows hike

  • Timehalf day
  • SettingHeat in summer; shade is limited at midday.
  • TicketsEntrance pass; seasonal shuttle required in the main canyon.
  • Get therePark in Springdale or visitor area and ride the shuttle.
