🦏 San Diego Zoo Safari Park is a classic safari park stop in Escondido, California. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

Your printable kit focuses on Reticulated giraffe, African elephant, Zebra, African lion, Cheetah, and Western lowland gorilla — a manageable set for young kids through about age 10–11, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find an animal on open grass; Spot a herd or group; Find stripes or a long neck; Watch something walk slowly. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

For San Diego Zoo Safari Park, arrive with the one-page hunt in your bag, pick a first target from the shortlist, and treat the rest as bonus — a calm day beats a complete checklist.

Do the Africa tram (or equivalent) early, then walk gorillas and cats. 1Less keeps the promise simple: one less decision before a museum or zoo day with kids.
