🦈 Shark

Sharks in signature tanks.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why do sharks have so many teeth? Do they ever sleep?
Teeth fall out and new ones grow in. Sharks rest, but not like we sleep in a bed.

🐢 Sea turtle

Sea turtles on the loop.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Plants / leaves, Both
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why do sea turtles have flippers instead of feet?
Flippers are for swimming far. Feet would be better on land, but sea turtles live in the water.

🎐 Jellyfish

Soft glow for a quieter moment.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Both, Meat
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why can a jellyfish sting? Does it have a brain?
The sting catches food. A jellyfish has no brain — just a simple body that drifts and pulses.

射线 Stingray

Flat swimmers kids love to watch.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Both
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why is a stingray so flat? Where is its mouth?
A flat body hides on the sand. The mouth is on the bottom, so it can eat what it finds there.

🐧 African penguin

Penguins if on exhibit that day.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet, Cold / snow
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why can’t penguins fly? Why the black-and-white coat?
Their wings became flippers for swimming. Dark backs and white bellies help them hide from above and below.

🌊 Seahorse

Tiny upright swimmer that holds on with its tail.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Both, Insects
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Hide, Swim
Why does a seahorse swim upright? Who carries the babies?
An upright body and a holding tail fit in sea grass. The dad carries the eggs in a pouch.
