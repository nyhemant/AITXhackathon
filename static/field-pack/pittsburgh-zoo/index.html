🦈 Shark

Sharks indoors.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why do sharks have so many teeth? Do they ever sleep?
Teeth fall out and new ones grow in. Sharks rest, but not like we sleep in a bed.

🐘 African elephant

Elephants outdoors.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Plants / leaves
Where is home?
Grassland, Forest
What is their superpower?
Stomp / strong, Swim
Why do elephants have huge ears? What is a trunk for?
Big ears let heat out, like fans. The trunk smells, grabs food, drinks, and says hello.

🦒 Giraffe

Giraffes.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Plants / leaves
Where is home?
Grassland
What is their superpower?
Run fast, Stomp / strong
Why is a giraffe’s neck so long? How does it drink?
A long neck reaches high leaves. To drink, a giraffe spreads its front legs and bends down carefully.

Where is home?
Water / wet

Why can a jellyfish sting? Does it have a brain?
The sting catches food. A jellyfish has no brain — just a simple body that drifts and pulses.

🐢 Sea turtle

Shelled ocean traveler — flippers for swimming.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Plants / leaves, Both
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why do sea turtles have flippers instead of feet?
Flippers are for swimming far. Feet would be better on land, but sea turtles live in the water.

🐧 Penguin

Waddle on land, zoom in water.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Fish
Where is home?
Water / wet, Cold / snow
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why can’t penguins fly? Why the black-and-white coat?
Their wings became flippers for swimming. Dark backs and white bellies help them hide from above and below.

Talk & Q&A

What do they eat?
Meat, Both
Where is home?
Water / wet
What is their superpower?
Swim, Hide
Why is a stingray so flat? Where is its mouth?
A flat body hides on the sand. The mouth is on the bottom, so it can eat what it finds there.
