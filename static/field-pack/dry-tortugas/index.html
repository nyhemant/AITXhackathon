Animals you might meet

In the water around the keys. Sightings aren’t promised. Give wildlife lots of space — keep a long, quiet distance.

🐢 Sea turtle

The keys were named for turtles — if you see one, keep a long, quiet distance.

🦈 Shark

Nurse sharks sometimes rest in the shallows — watch from a boat or snorkel and give lots of space.
