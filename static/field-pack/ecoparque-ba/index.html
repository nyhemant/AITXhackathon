🦩 Ecoparque Buenos Aires is a classic zoo stop in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

The shortlist highlights Caribbean flamingo, African penguin, Galápagos tortoise, Ring-tailed lemur, Two-toed sloth, and Ostrich. Each pick has a kid-sized blurb so a preschooler or early elementary explorer can understand why it is cool before you arrive.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find an animal walking free (not in a big cage); Spot a bird with bright feathers; Find a pond or water area; Notice three different plants. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

For Ecoparque Buenos Aires, arrive with the one-page hunt in your bag, pick a first target from the shortlist, and treat the rest as bonus — a calm day beats a complete checklist.

Expect a greener, quieter pace; check what’s open that day. Field Trip Kit is free to use. Open the full interactive list when you want optional Q&A cards, or use the one-page hunt alone for a lighter outing.
