Animals you might meet

Along the boardwalks and sloughs — and sometimes the far coast. Stay on the path. Sightings aren’t promised. Give wildlife lots of space — stay far from the water edge.

🐊 American alligator

Sometimes along boardwalks and sloughs — stay on the path and far from the water edge.

🐢 Sea turtle

Far coast and Cape Sable beaches — not the Anhinga boardwalk. Never promised.
