🐨 Taronga Zoo is a classic zoo stop in Sydney, Australia. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

Instead of racing through every exhibit, start with top picks like Koala, Sumatran tiger, Reticulated giraffe, African lion, Western lowland gorilla, and Red panda. That keeps toddlers and school-age kids engaged without overwhelm.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck or trunk. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Practical tip for Sydney: print the hunt the night before, pack a pen, and start with one or two favorites near the entrance so early energy pays off.

Treasure hunt checklist (printable)

Sample finds from the free one-page hunt for Taronga Zoo:

  1. Find an animal taller than a grown-up
  2. Spot stripes or spots
  3. Watch something swim or splash
  4. Find a long neck or trunk
  5. Point toward the harbour if you can see it
  6. Find a bird that cannot fly far
  7. Hear a loud sound (or a quiet one)
  8. Photo your favorite animal
