🐘 Oregon Zoo is a classic zoo stop in Portland, OR. Families use this free printable scavenger hunt to give little explorers a clear mission without overpacking the day.

Your printable kit focuses on African elephant, Reticulated giraffe, African lion, Sumatran tiger, Western lowland gorilla, and African penguin — a manageable set for Pre-K through about grade 5, with room to skip anything that is closed or crowded.

On the hunt sheet, kids can check off finds such as Find an animal taller than a grown-up; Spot stripes or spots; Watch something swim or splash; Find a long neck, trunk, or horns. Grown-ups stay free to enjoy the day; the paper does the guiding.

Weather or crowds in OR can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.
