Visiting Georgia Aquarium with kids does not have to mean wandering until everyone is tired. This free one-page scavenger hunt for Atlanta, Georgia keeps the day focused and fun.

The shortlist highlights Shark, Stingray, Sea turtle, Jellyfish, African penguin, and Asian small-clawed otter. Each pick has a kid-sized blurb so a preschooler or early elementary explorer can understand why it is cool before you arrive.

The treasure hunt is one page: check boxes as you go. Sample challenges include Find something with fins; Spot a flat swimmer (like a ray); Find a shell or hard cover; Watch bubbles or moving water. There is no score and no app required on site — print before you leave home or save the page for later.

Weather or crowds in GA can change plans. The shortlist and hunt are designed so you can reorder stops without losing the fun.

Start at the biggest tank (whale sharks), then belugas and penguins. Field Trip Kit is free to use. Open the full interactive list when you want optional Q&A cards, or use the one-page hunt alone for a lighter outing.
