💧 Plitvice Lakes National Park

Plitvice — boardwalk lakes and waterfalls; one entrance loop, not every trail.

  • Timefull day
  • SettingLots of walking and stairs; strollers hard on boardwalks.
  • TicketsTimed tickets often required; buy ahead in peak season.
  • Get thereTwo main entrances; buses from Zagreb/Zadar.
