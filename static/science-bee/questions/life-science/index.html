Life Science Science Bee Questions for Kids

Alive or not alive, life cycles, food chains, habitats and the big animal groups. 41 questions in two age bands, each with the answer, a gentle hint and a kind “here’s why” line.

Play a living things round Little: Pre-K–K Big: 1st–3rd

Read a question out loud and let the kids answer before you open the answer. Little kids: read the choices too.

Little: Pre-K to K (19 questions)

  1. Is a rock alive?

    Choices: No · Yes

    Answer

    No

    Hint: Does a rock eat, grow, or breathe?

    Why: A rock is not alive. Living things grow, need food and water, and make more of their kind.

  2. Is a tree alive?

    Choices: Yes · No

    Answer

    Yes

    Hint: Does a tree grow bigger every year?

    Why: A tree is alive. It grows, drinks water through its roots, and makes seeds.

  3. What do all living things need to stay alive?

    Choices: Toys · Shoes · Water

    Answer

    Water

    Hint: You drink it when you are thirsty.

    Why: Every living thing needs water. Toys and shoes are nice, but plants and animals need water.

  4. A baby frog that swims and has a tail is called a...

    Choices: Tadpole · Chick · Puppy

    Answer

    Tadpole

    Hint: It starts with T, like tail.

    Why: A baby frog is a tadpole. It swims with a tail, then grows legs and becomes a frog.

  5. What does a caterpillar turn into?

    Choices: A bird · A fish · A butterfly

    Answer

    A butterfly

    Hint: It has colorful wings.

    Why: A caterpillar grows, makes a chrysalis, and comes out as a butterfly.

  6. What hatches out of a chicken egg?

    Choices: A calf · A kitten · A chick

    Answer

    A chick

    Hint: It says peep peep.

    Why: A baby chicken, called a chick, hatches out of the egg.

  7. Which animal has feathers?

    Choices: A fish · A dog · A bird

    Answer

    A bird

    Hint: It might sing in a tree.

    Why: Birds are the only animals with feathers.

  8. Where does a fish live?

    Choices: In the sand dunes · In a tree · In water

    Answer

    In water

    Hint: Splash!

    Why: Fish live in water and breathe with gills.

  9. Which one is a baby dog?

    Choices: A lamb · A puppy · A cub

    Answer

    A puppy

    Hint: It can wag a tiny tail.

    Why: A baby dog is a puppy. A lamb is a baby sheep, and a cub can be a baby bear or lion.

  10. What do you call an animal that eats only plants?

    Choices: A plant eater · A meat eater

    Answer

    A plant eater

    Hint: Think of a cow munching grass.

    Why: An animal that eats only plants is a plant eater, also called an herbivore. Cows and rabbits are plant eaters.

  11. Which animal is a mammal with fur?

    Choices: A snake · A bear · A frog

    Answer

    A bear

    Hint: It is fuzzy.

    Why: A bear has fur and feeds its babies milk, so it is a mammal. Snakes have scales and frogs have smooth skin.

  12. Which one grows into a big oak tree?

    Choices: An acorn · A pebble · A shell

    Answer

    An acorn

    Hint: Squirrels like to bury it.

    Why: An acorn is the seed of an oak tree. Plant it and a tiny oak can grow.

  13. Which home is a bird's?

    Choices: A web · A nest · A hive

    Answer

    A nest

    Hint: Birds build it out of twigs.

    Why: Many birds build nests for their eggs. Spiders make webs, and bees live in hives.

  14. Which animal lays eggs?

    Choices: A horse · A cow · A duck

    Answer

    A duck

    Hint: Quack quack.

    Why: Ducks lay eggs. Cows and horses have babies that are born alive.

  15. What do baby mammals drink from their mother?

    Choices: Soda · Juice · Milk

    Answer

    Milk

    Hint: It is white and comes from mom.

    Why: Baby mammals drink milk from their mothers. That is one thing all mammals have in common.

  16. Is a toy robot alive?

    Choices: Yes · No

    Answer

    No

    Hint: Does it grow up and have babies?

    Why: A toy robot is not alive. It cannot grow, eat, or make babies, even if it moves.

  17. Which animal changes from a tadpole to a grown-up?

    Choices: A cat · A frog · An owl

    Answer

    A frog

    Hint: Ribbit!

    Why: Frogs start as tadpoles in the water and grow into frogs.

  18. Which of these lived long, long ago and is now gone?

    Choices: Cats · Dinosaurs · Dogs

    Answer

    Dinosaurs

    Hint: T. rex was one.

    Why: Dinosaurs lived a very long time ago. People learn about them from fossils.

  19. 🐸

    This animal hops and says ribbit. What was it when it was a baby?

    Choices: A tadpole · A chick · A puppy

    Answer

    A tadpole

    Hint: It swam in a pond with a tail.

    Why: A frog starts as a tadpole.

Big: 1st to 3rd grade (22 questions)

  1. A living thing that makes its own food from sunlight is called a...

    Choices: Consumer · Producer · Decomposer

    Answer

    Producer

    Hint: It produces, or makes, food.

    Why: A producer makes its own food. Green plants are producers because they use sunlight to make food.

  2. Mushrooms and worms that break down dead leaves are called...

    Choices: Producers · Decomposers · Predators

    Answer

    Decomposers

    Hint: They de-compose, or break things down.

    Why: Decomposers break down dead plants and animals and return nutrients to the soil.

  3. An animal that hunts other animals for food is called a...

    Choices: Prey · Producer · Predator

    Answer

    Predator

    Hint: A hawk chasing a mouse is one.

    Why: A predator hunts other animals. The animal being hunted is called prey.

  4. In a food chain, grass is eaten by a rabbit. What might eat the rabbit?

    Choices: The sun · A fox · A carrot

    Answer

    A fox

    Hint: Think of an animal that eats meat.

    Why: A fox could eat the rabbit. Food chains go from plants to plant eaters to meat eaters.

  5. What do you call the tiny building blocks that make up every living thing?

    Choices: Pebbles · Cells · Atoms of gold

    Answer

    Cells

    Hint: You need a microscope to see most of them.

    Why: Cells are the tiny building blocks of living things. Your body is made of trillions of them.

  6. What are the four stages in a butterfly's life cycle?

    Choices: Egg, caterpillar, chrysalis, butterfly · Seed, sprout, flower, fruit · Egg, tadpole, froglet, frog

    Answer

    Egg, caterpillar, chrysalis, butterfly

    Hint: It starts as an egg on a leaf.

    Why: A butterfly goes from egg to caterpillar to chrysalis to butterfly. That big change is called metamorphosis.

  7. What is the big word for when an animal changes body shape as it grows, like a caterpillar becoming a butterfly?

    Choices: Migration · Hibernation · Metamorphosis

    Answer

    Metamorphosis

    Hint: It starts with meta.

    Why: Metamorphosis means a big change in body form, like caterpillar to butterfly or tadpole to frog.

  8. Animals with a backbone are called...

    Choices: Insects · Vertebrates · Invertebrates

    Answer

    Vertebrates

    Hint: Vertebra is a bone in your back.

    Why: Animals with backbones are vertebrates. Fish, frogs, snakes, birds and mammals all have backbones.

  9. Which animal is an invertebrate, with no backbone?

    Choices: A parrot · A shark · A jellyfish

    Answer

    A jellyfish

    Hint: It is soft and wobbly.

    Why: A jellyfish has no backbone, so it is an invertebrate. Sharks and parrots have backbones.

  10. Which group of animals has smooth, damp skin and usually starts life in water?

    Choices: Amphibians · Birds · Reptiles

    Answer

    Amphibians

    Hint: Frogs and salamanders belong here.

    Why: Amphibians, like frogs and salamanders, have damp skin and usually start life in water.

  11. Snakes, lizards and turtles have dry, scaly skin. What group are they in?

    Choices: Reptiles · Amphibians · Mammals

    Answer

    Reptiles

    Hint: Crocodiles are in this group too.

    Why: They are reptiles. Reptiles have dry, scaly skin.

  12. A dolphin breathes air and feeds its babies milk. What kind of animal is it?

    Choices: A mammal · A fish · An amphibian

    Answer

    A mammal

    Hint: Dogs and people are in this group too.

    Why: A dolphin is a mammal, not a fish. It comes up to breathe air through its blowhole.

  13. What is it called when an animal's color helps it blend in with its surroundings?

    Choices: Echolocation · Camouflage · Pollination

    Answer

    Camouflage

    Hint: Soldiers wear clothes with this pattern.

    Why: That is camouflage. It helps animals hide from predators or sneak up on prey.

  14. When a kind of animal is gone forever and none are left alive, it is...

    Choices: Endangered · Extinct · Asleep

    Answer

    Extinct

    Hint: Dinosaurs (other than birds) are this.

    Why: An extinct animal has no living members left. Endangered means there are only a few left.

  15. Scientists say birds are living relatives of which animals?

    Choices: Insects · Sharks · Dinosaurs

    Answer

    Dinosaurs

    Hint: Think of a T. rex and a chicken.

    Why: Birds come from a group of dinosaurs. That is why scientists call birds living dinosaurs.

  16. What is the place where an animal naturally lives and finds food, water and shelter?

    Choices: Orbit · Garage · Habitat

    Answer

    Habitat

    Hint: A pond is one for a frog.

    Why: A habitat is an animal's natural home. A pond, a forest and a desert are all habitats.

  17. What tool helps scientists see things too tiny for their eyes, like cells?

    Choices: A telescope · A thermometer · A microscope

    Answer

    A microscope

    Hint: Micro means very small.

    Why: A microscope makes tiny things look bigger. A telescope is for faraway things like stars.

  18. A frog egg hatches into a tadpole. What does the tadpole breathe with at first?

    Choices: Feathers · Lungs · Gills

    Answer

    Gills

    Hint: Fish breathe with these too.

    Why: Young tadpoles breathe with gills, like fish. As they grow into frogs, most grow lungs.

  19. Which scientist studies living things?

    Choices: An astronomer · A geologist · A biologist

    Answer

    A biologist

    Hint: Bio means life.

    Why: A biologist studies living things. Geologists study rocks, and astronomers study space.

  20. An animal that eats both plants and meat is called...

    Choices: An omnivore · An herbivore · A carnivore

    Answer

    An omnivore

    Hint: Omni means all.

    Why: An omnivore eats both plants and animals. Bears and people are omnivores.

  21. 🐬

    This animal lives in the ocean. Is it a fish or a mammal?

    Choices: An amphibian · A mammal · A fish

    Answer

    A mammal

    Hint: It breathes air through a blowhole.

    Why: A dolphin is a mammal. It breathes air and feeds its babies milk.

  22. 🕷️

    Is this animal an insect?

    Choices: Yes, it is an insect · No, it is a reptile · No, it is an arachnid

    Answer

    No, it is an arachnid

    Hint: Count its legs: there are eight.

    Why: Spiders have eight legs, so they are arachnids, not insects.
