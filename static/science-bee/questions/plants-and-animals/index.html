Plants and Animals Science Bee Questions for Kids

Plant parts and pollination, animal babies, record-setting animals and clever adaptations. 51 questions in two age bands, each with the answer, a gentle hint and a kind “here’s why” line.

Play a plants & animals round Little: Pre-K–K Big: 1st–3rd

Read a question out loud and let the kids answer before you open the answer. Little kids: read the choices too.

Little: Pre-K to K (25 questions)

  1. What part of a plant grows under the ground and drinks water?

    Choices: The leaf · The roots · The flower

    Answer

    The roots

    Hint: They hold the plant in the soil.

    Why: Roots grow underground, hold the plant in place and soak up water.

  2. What do plants need from the sky to grow?

    Choices: Moonlight only · Fireworks · Sunlight

    Answer

    Sunlight

    Hint: It is bright and warm.

    Why: Plants need sunlight to make their food.

  3. What color are most leaves in summer?

    Choices: Black · Green · Purple

    Answer

    Green

    Hint: Like grass.

    Why: Most leaves are green in summer.

  4. What do bees visit to collect nectar?

    Choices: Flowers · Cars · Rocks

    Answer

    Flowers

    Hint: They are colorful and smell nice.

    Why: Bees visit flowers for nectar and carry pollen from flower to flower.

  5. What does a seed need to start growing?

    Choices: A hat · Water · A song

    Answer

    Water

    Hint: Plants get thirsty too.

    Why: A seed needs water and warmth to sprout.

  6. Which animal has a very long neck?

    Choices: A giraffe · A duck · A pig

    Answer

    A giraffe

    Hint: It eats leaves from tall trees.

    Why: The giraffe has a very long neck and is the tallest land animal.

  7. Which animal has a trunk?

    Choices: A lion · A zebra · An elephant

    Answer

    An elephant

    Hint: It is big and gray.

    Why: An elephant uses its trunk to smell, drink and grab food.

  8. Which animal hops and carries its baby in a pouch?

    Choices: A kangaroo · A turtle · A horse

    Answer

    A kangaroo

    Hint: It lives in Australia.

    Why: Kangaroo moms carry their babies, called joeys, in a pouch.

  9. Which animal has black and white stripes?

    Choices: A flamingo · A zebra · A frog

    Answer

    A zebra

    Hint: It looks like a striped horse.

    Why: A zebra has black and white stripes.

  10. How many legs does an insect have?

    Choices: Six · Two · Eight

    Answer

    Six

    Hint: Count the legs on an ant.

    Why: Insects have six legs. Spiders have eight, so spiders are not insects.

  11. How many legs does a spider have?

    Choices: Six · Eight · Four

    Answer

    Eight

    Hint: Same as an octopus has arms.

    Why: Spiders have eight legs.

  12. Which animal is awake at night and says hoo-hoo?

    Choices: An owl · A cow · A rooster

    Answer

    An owl

    Hint: It has big eyes.

    Why: Most owls are awake at night. Animals that are busy at night are called nocturnal.

  13. What does a cactus have to protect itself?

    Choices: Feathers · Spines · Fur

    Answer

    Spines

    Hint: Ouch, they are pointy!

    Why: A cactus has sharp spines. It also stores water in its thick stem.

  14. What does a baby cat get called?

    Choices: A kitten · A puppy · A cub

    Answer

    A kitten

    Hint: Meow!

    Why: A baby cat is a kitten.

  15. Which animal has a shell it can hide in?

    Choices: A dog · A turtle · A bird

    Answer

    A turtle

    Hint: It moves slowly.

    Why: A turtle can pull its head into its hard shell.

  16. Which fruit grows on a tree: an apple or a carrot?

    Choices: A carrot · An apple

    Answer

    An apple

    Hint: A carrot grows in the ground.

    Why: Apples grow on trees. Carrots are roots that grow underground.

  17. Which bird cannot fly but is a great swimmer?

    Choices: A robin · An eagle · A penguin

    Answer

    A penguin

    Hint: It waddles on the ice.

    Why: Penguins cannot fly, but they swim very well with their flippers.

  18. What do caterpillars mostly eat?

    Choices: Meat · Rocks · Leaves

    Answer

    Leaves

    Hint: Munch munch on a plant.

    Why: Most caterpillars munch on leaves.

  19. 🦋

    What will this bug flutter around looking for?

    Choices: Rocks · Flowers · Snowballs

    Answer

    Flowers

    Hint: It sips sweet nectar.

    Why: Butterflies visit flowers to sip nectar.

  20. 🌵

    Where does this prickly plant grow best?

    Choices: On an iceberg · Under the ocean · In a dry desert

    Answer

    In a dry desert

    Hint: It can go a long time without rain.

    Why: Cactus plants grow well in dry deserts. They store water in their stems.

  21. 🐧

    Can this bird fly?

    Choices: No · Yes

    Answer

    No

    Hint: It uses its flippers to swim.

    Why: Penguins cannot fly. They are excellent swimmers.

  22. 🦒

    What does this animal reach with its long neck?

    Choices: Fish in the sea · Leaves high in trees · Worms underground

    Answer

    Leaves high in trees

    Hint: It is the tallest land animal.

    Why: A giraffe stretches its long neck to eat leaves high in the trees.

  23. Look at the plant. What is the part under the ground called?

    Choices: Petals · Roots · Leaves

    Answer

    Roots

    Hint: It is marked number 1.

    Why: The part under the ground is the roots. They drink up water.

  24. What is this big animal's long nose called?

    Choices: A beak · A tail · A trunk

    Answer

    A trunk

    Hint: It can spray water with it.

    Why: An elephant's long nose is called a trunk.

  25. 🐙

    How many arms does this animal have?

    Choices: Eight · Two · Three

    Answer

    Eight

    Hint: Count to eight!

    Why: An octopus has eight arms.

Big: 1st to 3rd grade (26 questions)

  1. What is the process plants use to make food from sunlight, water and air?

    Choices: Hibernation · Photosynthesis · Pollination

    Answer

    Photosynthesis

    Hint: Photo means light.

    Why: Photosynthesis is how plants use sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make food.

  2. What green material in leaves captures sunlight?

    Choices: Nectar · Pollen · Chlorophyll

    Answer

    Chlorophyll

    Hint: It makes leaves look green.

    Why: Chlorophyll is the green material that captures sunlight for photosynthesis.

  3. What gas do plants give off that animals need to breathe?

    Choices: Carbon dioxide · Oxygen · Helium

    Answer

    Oxygen

    Hint: You breathe it in.

    Why: Plants give off oxygen during photosynthesis.

  4. What yellow dust do bees carry from flower to flower?

    Choices: Sand · Pollen · Chalk

    Answer

    Pollen

    Hint: It can make people sneeze.

    Why: Bees carry pollen between flowers. That helps flowers make seeds.

  5. Moving pollen from one flower to another is called...

    Choices: Migration · Photosynthesis · Pollination

    Answer

    Pollination

    Hint: It starts like pollen.

    Why: That is pollination. Bees, butterflies, birds and wind can all pollinate flowers.

  6. What is the long, deep winter sleep some animals take called?

    Choices: Migration · Hibernation · Camouflage

    Answer

    Hibernation

    Hint: Some bears and ground squirrels do it.

    Why: Hibernation is a long, deep rest through the winter that saves energy.

  7. When birds fly south for the winter and come back in spring, it is called...

    Choices: Migration · Hibernation · Metamorphosis

    Answer

    Migration

    Hint: Many geese do this every year.

    Why: Migration is a long trip animals make each year, often to find food or warmer weather.

  8. Trees that lose all their leaves in the fall are called...

    Choices: Deciduous · Cactus · Evergreen

    Answer

    Deciduous

    Hint: It is the opposite of evergreen.

    Why: Deciduous trees drop their leaves in fall. Evergreens like pine trees keep their needles.

  9. Bats find their way in the dark by making sounds and listening for echoes. What is this called?

    Choices: Camouflage · Echolocation · Photosynthesis

    Answer

    Echolocation

    Hint: Echo plus location.

    Why: Echolocation means using echoes to find things. Bats and dolphins use it.

  10. What is the fastest land animal?

    Choices: The horse · The lion · The cheetah

    Answer

    The cheetah

    Hint: It is a spotted big cat.

    Why: The cheetah is the fastest land animal over short sprints.

  11. What is the largest animal ever known to live on Earth?

    Choices: The African elephant · T. rex · The blue whale

    Answer

    The blue whale

    Hint: It lives in the ocean.

    Why: The blue whale is the largest animal known to have ever lived.

  12. What is the largest fish in the world?

    Choices: The blue whale · The whale shark · The great white shark

    Answer

    The whale shark

    Hint: Careful, a whale is not a fish.

    Why: The whale shark is the largest fish. A blue whale is bigger, but it is a mammal.

  13. A camel's hump stores mostly what?

    Choices: Sand · Fat · Water

    Answer

    Fat

    Hint: It is energy for long trips.

    Why: A camel's hump stores fat, not water. The fat is energy for long desert trips.

  14. What are a kangaroo's babies called?

    Choices: Joeys · Cubs · Kids

    Answer

    Joeys

    Hint: Koalas have them too.

    Why: Baby kangaroos are joeys. Baby koalas are also called joeys.

  15. Animals like kangaroos and koalas whose babies grow in a pouch are called...

    Choices: Marsupials · Reptiles · Amphibians

    Answer

    Marsupials

    Hint: It starts with M.

    Why: Kangaroos and koalas are marsupials, mammals whose babies grow in a pouch.

  16. How many arms does an octopus have?

    Choices: Eight · Six · Ten

    Answer

    Eight

    Hint: Octo means eight.

    Why: An octopus has eight arms.

  17. A sea star is not a fish. What does it use to move along the sea floor?

    Choices: Tiny tube feet · Wings · Fins

    Answer

    Tiny tube feet

    Hint: They are under its arms.

    Why: Sea stars move on hundreds of tiny tube feet under their arms.

  18. What do you call the part of a plant that grows into a new plant?

    Choices: A leaf vein · A thorn · A seed

    Answer

    A seed

    Hint: An acorn is one.

    Why: A seed holds a baby plant and food to help it start growing.

  19. Which part of a plant carries water up from the roots to the leaves?

    Choices: The flower · The petal · The stem

    Answer

    The stem

    Hint: On a tree it is the trunk.

    Why: The stem carries water up and holds the plant up. A tree trunk is a woody stem.

  20. Animals that are busy at night and sleep during the day are called...

    Choices: Deciduous · Nocturnal · Diurnal

    Answer

    Nocturnal

    Hint: Nocturne is a night song.

    Why: Nocturnal animals, like most owls and bats, are active at night.

  21. Look at the plant. Which part, number 3, makes seeds?

    Choices: The root · The stem · The flower

    Answer

    The flower

    Hint: Bees visit it.

    Why: The flower makes seeds after it is pollinated.

  22. This giant ocean animal has spots and filters tiny food from the water. Is it a whale or a shark?

    Choices: A dolphin · A whale, so it is a mammal · A shark, so it is a fish

    Answer

    A shark, so it is a fish

    Hint: Its name has both words in it.

    Why: It is a whale shark, the largest fish in the world.

  23. This flat animal flaps fins like wings. Is a manta ray a fish or a mammal?

    Choices: A fish · A bird · A mammal

    Answer

    A fish

    Hint: It breathes with gills.

    Why: A manta ray is a fish. It breathes underwater with gills.

  24. 🐝

    What does this insect carry from flower to flower?

    Choices: Rain · Sand · Pollen

    Answer

    Pollen

    Hint: It is yellow dust.

    Why: Bees carry pollen, which helps flowers make seeds.

  25. 🦇

    How does this animal find bugs in the dark?

    Choices: Photosynthesis · A flashlight · Echolocation

    Answer

    Echolocation

    Hint: It listens for echoes.

    Why: Bats make sounds and listen for echoes to find food in the dark.

  26. 🍄

    Mushrooms are not plants. What group are they in?

    Choices: Fungi · Insects · Mammals

    Answer

    Fungi

    Hint: Yeast is in the same group.

    Why: Mushrooms are fungi, like yeast and mold.
