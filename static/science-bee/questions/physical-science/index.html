Physical Science Science Bee Questions for Kids

Solids, liquids and gases, magnets, gravity, light, sound, simple machines and circuits. 43 questions in two age bands, each with the answer, a gentle hint and a kind “here’s why” line.

Play a matter & forces round Little: Pre-K–K Big: 1st–3rd

Read a question out loud and let the kids answer before you open the answer. Little kids: read the choices too.

Little: Pre-K to K (20 questions)

  1. What happens to an ice cube on a warm, sunny day?

    Choices: It grows · It turns to stone · It melts

    Answer

    It melts

    Hint: It turns into a puddle.

    Why: Warmth melts ice. The solid ice turns into liquid water.

  2. What sticks to a magnet?

    Choices: A plastic spoon · A steel paper clip · A wooden block

    Answer

    A steel paper clip

    Hint: It is made of metal.

    Why: Magnets pull on things with iron in them, like a steel paper clip. Wood and plastic do not stick.

  3. If you drop a ball, which way does it go?

    Choices: Sideways forever · Up · Down

    Answer

    Down

    Hint: Gravity pulls it.

    Why: Gravity pulls things down toward the ground.

  4. Will a rock sink or float in water?

    Choices: Sink · Float

    Answer

    Sink

    Hint: Plop!

    Why: A rock sinks because it is heavy for its size.

  5. What do you get when water freezes?

    Choices: Sand · Steam · Ice

    Answer

    Ice

    Hint: It is cold and slippery.

    Why: When water gets cold enough it freezes into ice.

  6. Is a push or a pull what you use to open a drawer toward you?

    Choices: A push · A pull

    Answer

    A pull

    Hint: You bring it closer to you.

    Why: Bringing something toward you is a pull. Pushes move things away.

  7. What do you see when light is blocked by your body on a sunny day?

    Choices: A shadow · A puddle · A rainbow

    Answer

    A shadow

    Hint: It follows you around.

    Why: Your body blocks the light and makes a shadow.

  8. Which is a liquid?

    Choices: A block · Milk · A spoon

    Answer

    Milk

    Hint: You can pour it.

    Why: Milk is a liquid. Liquids can be poured and take the shape of their cup.

  9. Which one makes a sound when you hit it?

    Choices: A drum · A cotton ball · A pillow

    Answer

    A drum

    Hint: Boom boom!

    Why: A drum makes a loud sound when you hit it because it shakes, or vibrates.

  10. Which is easier to roll across the floor?

    Choices: A box · A ball

    Answer

    A ball

    Hint: It is round.

    Why: A round ball rolls easily. A box slides or tips instead.

  11. What makes a flashlight shine?

    Choices: A sock · Water · A battery

    Answer

    A battery

    Hint: It gives the light energy.

    Why: A battery gives electricity to the bulb so it lights up.

  12. Which is the hottest?

    Choices: An ice pop · A snowman · A stove burner that is on

    Answer

    A stove burner that is on

    Hint: Never touch it!

    Why: A stove that is on is very hot. Ice pops and snowmen are cold.

  13. What does a mirror do with light?

    Choices: Bounces it back · Eats it · Makes it wet

    Answer

    Bounces it back

    Hint: You can see yourself in it.

    Why: A mirror reflects, or bounces back, light. That is how you see yourself.

  14. What comes out of a pot of very hot, boiling water?

    Choices: Sand · Ice · Steam

    Answer

    Steam

    Hint: It looks like a little cloud.

    Why: Boiling water turns into steam, a gas. Stay back, steam is hot!

  15. Which is heavier: a bowling ball or a feather?

    Choices: A feather · A bowling ball

    Answer

    A bowling ball

    Hint: Which one would be hard to lift?

    Why: A bowling ball is much heavier than a feather.

  16. What do you call something solid, like a block, that keeps its own shape?

    Choices: A liquid · A gas · A solid

    Answer

    A solid

    Hint: It does not spill.

    Why: A solid keeps its shape. Liquids spill and gases spread out.

  17. What sound does a tight rubber band make when you pluck it?

    Choices: A twang · Nothing at all

    Answer

    A twang

    Hint: It wiggles fast.

    Why: A plucked rubber band vibrates and makes a twang sound.

  18. What helps a sled slide down a snowy hill?

    Choices: Gravity · Sunlight · Glue

    Answer

    Gravity

    Hint: It is the same pull that makes things fall.

    Why: Gravity pulls the sled down the hill.

  19. Will this magnet pull the paper clip toward it?

    Choices: Yes · No

    Answer

    Yes

    Hint: The paper clip is made of steel.

    Why: Yes! A magnet pulls on steel paper clips.

  20. 🧊

    What happens to ice when it gets warm?

    Choices: It turns into a rock · It gets colder · It melts into water

    Answer

    It melts into water

    Hint: Drip drip.

    Why: Warm ice melts into liquid water.

Big: 1st to 3rd grade (23 questions)

  1. What are the three common states of matter?

    Choices: Solid, liquid and gas · Hot, warm and cold · Big, medium and small

    Answer

    Solid, liquid and gas

    Hint: Ice, water and steam.

    Why: Matter is usually a solid, a liquid or a gas, like ice, water and steam.

  2. What is it called when liquid water turns into a gas?

    Choices: Freezing · Condensation · Evaporation

    Answer

    Evaporation

    Hint: A puddle disappears on a sunny day.

    Why: That is evaporation. Heat turns liquid water into water vapor.

  3. Water drops form on the outside of a cold glass of lemonade. What is this called?

    Choices: Melting · Evaporation · Condensation

    Answer

    Condensation

    Hint: Water vapor in the air turns back into liquid.

    Why: That is condensation. Water vapor from the air cools on the glass and becomes liquid drops.

  4. At sea level, water boils at 100 degrees on which temperature scale?

    Choices: Kelvin · Celsius · Fahrenheit

    Answer

    Celsius

    Hint: The scale is named after Anders Celsius.

    Why: Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level. That is 212 degrees Fahrenheit.

  5. What force pulls objects toward Earth?

    Choices: Magnetism · Gravity · Friction

    Answer

    Gravity

    Hint: It is why apples fall from trees.

    Why: Gravity pulls objects toward Earth. It also keeps the Moon going around Earth.

  6. What force slows a ball rolling across grass?

    Choices: Gravity pulling up · Friction · Magnetism

    Answer

    Friction

    Hint: Rubbing between two surfaces.

    Why: Friction happens when surfaces rub, and it slows things down. It is stronger on rough grass than on a smooth floor.

  7. The ends of a bar magnet are called its...

    Choices: Corners · Poles · Roots

    Answer

    Poles

    Hint: Like the North and South ones on Earth.

    Why: A magnet has a north pole and a south pole.

  8. If you put the north poles of two magnets together, what happens?

    Choices: They push apart · They stick together · Nothing at all

    Answer

    They push apart

    Hint: Like poles do not like each other.

    Why: Like poles repel, or push apart. A north pole and a south pole attract.

  9. What is a ramp called as a simple machine?

    Choices: A pulley · A lever · An inclined plane

    Answer

    An inclined plane

    Hint: Incline means slope.

    Why: A ramp is an inclined plane. It lets you move a load up with less force.

  10. A seesaw is which kind of simple machine?

    Choices: A screw · A pulley · A lever

    Answer

    A lever

    Hint: It has a balance point in the middle.

    Why: A seesaw is a lever. The balance point is called the fulcrum.

  11. A rope over a wheel used to lift a flag up a pole is a...

    Choices: Ramp · Pulley · Wedge

    Answer

    Pulley

    Hint: Pull the rope down, the flag goes up.

    Why: That is a pulley. It can change the direction of a pull.

  12. Sound is made when something does what very quickly?

    Choices: Vibrates · Melts · Freezes

    Answer

    Vibrates

    Hint: Touch your throat while you hum.

    Why: Sound comes from vibrations, quick back-and-forth shakes.

  13. Which travels faster: light or sound?

    Choices: Sound · Light · They are the same speed

    Answer

    Light

    Hint: Think about seeing lightning before hearing thunder.

    Why: Light is much faster than sound. That is why you see lightning before you hear thunder.

  14. What is the path that electricity flows around, like battery to bulb and back, called?

    Choices: A circle dance · A circuit · An orbit

    Answer

    A circuit

    Hint: It sounds like circle.

    Why: A circuit is a complete loop. If the loop is broken, the bulb goes out.

  15. Which material is a good conductor that lets electricity flow through it?

    Choices: Copper · Wood · Rubber

    Answer

    Copper

    Hint: Wires are often made of it.

    Why: Copper is a metal that conducts electricity well. Rubber and wood are insulators.

  16. What is a material that blocks heat or electricity, like the rubber on a wire, called?

    Choices: A magnet · A conductor · An insulator

    Answer

    An insulator

    Hint: It keeps things in, like a warm blanket.

    Why: An insulator blocks the flow of heat or electricity.

  17. A glass prism splits white light into what?

    Choices: A rainbow of colors · Bubbles · Darkness

    Answer

    A rainbow of colors

    Hint: It happens with raindrops too.

    Why: A prism bends light and spreads white light into all the colors of the rainbow.

  18. When sugar disappears as you stir it into water, it...

    Choices: Freezes · Evaporates · Dissolves

    Answer

    Dissolves

    Hint: It mixes in so well you cannot see it.

    Why: Sugar dissolves in water. The water still tastes sweet because the sugar is still there.

  19. What kind of energy does a moving car, a rolling ball or a running dog have?

    Choices: Sound energy only · Motion energy · No energy

    Answer

    Motion energy

    Hint: Its science name is kinetic energy.

    Why: Anything moving has motion energy, also called kinetic energy.

  20. A balloon rubbed on your hair can stick to the wall. What is this?

    Choices: Static electricity · Gravity · Magnetism

    Answer

    Static electricity

    Hint: It can also make your hair stand up.

    Why: Rubbing builds up static electricity, which makes the balloon cling.

  21. Look at the circuit. Will the bulb light up?

    Choices: Yes, the loop is complete · No, batteries do not make light · No, the loop is broken

    Answer

    Yes, the loop is complete

    Hint: Follow the wire all the way around.

    Why: The wire makes a complete loop from the battery to the bulb and back, so it lights.

  22. Look at this circuit with a gap in the wire. Will the bulb light up?

    Choices: Yes, but only at night · No, the loop is broken · Yes, the loop is complete

    Answer

    No, the loop is broken

    Hint: Electricity needs a full loop.

    Why: There is a gap, so electricity cannot flow and the bulb stays dark.

  23. The sun is on the left. Which way does the shadow of the tree point?

    Choices: To the left, toward the sun · To the right, away from the sun · Straight up

    Answer

    To the right, away from the sun

    Hint: Shadows fall on the side away from the light.

    Why: The tree blocks sunlight, so the shadow points away from the sun.
