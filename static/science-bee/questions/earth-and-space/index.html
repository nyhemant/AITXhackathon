Earth and Space Science Bee Questions for Kids

The Sun, Moon and planets, rocks and volcanoes, oceans, glaciers and the stars. 44 questions in two age bands, each with the answer, a gentle hint and a kind “here’s why” line.

Play a earth & space round Little: Pre-K–K Big: 1st–3rd

Read a question out loud and let the kids answer before you open the answer. Little kids: read the choices too.

Little: Pre-K to K (21 questions)

  1. What big, bright star do you see in the daytime sky?

    Choices: A cloud · The Moon · The Sun

    Answer

    The Sun

    Hint: It keeps you warm.

    Why: The Sun is a star, and it is the one closest to Earth. Never look straight at it.

  2. What do you often see in the sky at night that changes shape over the month?

    Choices: The Moon · The Sun · A rainbow

    Answer

    The Moon

    Hint: Sometimes it looks like a banana, sometimes a ball.

    Why: The Moon looks like it changes shape. Those shapes are called phases.

  3. What is the name of the planet people live on?

    Choices: Mars · The Moon · Earth

    Answer

    Earth

    Hint: It is mostly blue with oceans.

    Why: People live on planet Earth.

  4. Which planet is called the Red Planet?

    Choices: Mars · Jupiter · Earth

    Answer

    Mars

    Hint: It starts with M.

    Why: Mars looks red because its dusty ground has rusty iron in it.

  5. Which planet has big, bright rings you can see in pictures?

    Choices: Earth · Mars · Saturn

    Answer

    Saturn

    Hint: It starts with S.

    Why: Saturn has the brightest, best-known rings, made of ice and rock.

  6. Who goes into space in a rocket?

    Choices: Firefighters · Astronauts · Chefs

    Answer

    Astronauts

    Hint: They wear big space suits.

    Why: Astronauts travel to space. They train for a long time first.

  7. What hot, melted rock comes out of a volcano?

    Choices: Juice · Snow · Lava

    Answer

    Lava

    Hint: It glows orange.

    Why: Lava is melted rock that flows out of a volcano.

  8. Is the ocean water salty or sweet?

    Choices: Salty · Sweet

    Answer

    Salty

    Hint: Do not drink it!

    Why: Ocean water is salty. Most rivers and lakes have fresh water.

  9. What do you call a very big, tall hill with a pointy top?

    Choices: A puddle · A valley · A mountain

    Answer

    A mountain

    Hint: People climb it.

    Why: A mountain is land that rises very high.

  10. When it is daytime, which way does the sun rise?

    Choices: In the east · In the west

    Answer

    In the east

    Hint: The opposite side from where it sets.

    Why: The sun rises in the east and sets in the west.

  11. Which is bigger, the Sun or the Earth?

    Choices: The Sun · The Earth

    Answer

    The Sun

    Hint: It only looks small because it is far away.

    Why: The Sun is much, much bigger than Earth. It looks small because it is so far away.

  12. What is a shape made by stars in the night sky called?

    Choices: A puddle · A constellation · A comet

    Answer

    A constellation

    Hint: The Big Dipper is part of one.

    Why: A constellation is a group of stars that people imagine as a picture.

  13. What do you call a rock or bone print from an animal that lived long ago?

    Choices: A fossil · A crystal · A coin

    Answer

    A fossil

    Hint: Dinosaur bones in a museum are these.

    Why: A fossil is what is left of a living thing from long ago, saved in rock.

  14. Where do you find sand, shells and waves?

    Choices: On a mountain top · At a beach · In a cave

    Answer

    At a beach

    Hint: You might build a sandcastle there.

    Why: A beach is where land meets water. Waves bring sand and shells.

  15. What is a hole in the ground where some animals like bats live called?

    Choices: A river · A cloud · A cave

    Answer

    A cave

    Hint: It is dark inside.

    Why: A cave is a big hollow space underground or in a hillside.

  16. Which is the hottest: the Sun, an ice cube or a snowball?

    Choices: An ice cube · The Sun · A snowball

    Answer

    The Sun

    Hint: It is a giant ball of hot gas.

    Why: The Sun is hottest. It is a huge ball of very hot glowing gas.

  17. Does the Moon make its own light?

    Choices: No, it reflects sunlight · Yes, it glows by itself

    Answer

    No, it reflects sunlight

    Hint: Think of a mirror.

    Why: The Moon does not make light. It shines because sunlight bounces off it.

  18. What is the ground shaking hard called?

    Choices: A snowfall · A rainbow · An earthquake

    Answer

    An earthquake

    Hint: Quake means shake.

    Why: An earthquake is when the ground shakes because rock deep underground moves.

  19. 🌋

    What is this mountain that can erupt?

    Choices: A lake · A cloud · A volcano

    Answer

    A volcano

    Hint: Hot lava can pour out of the top.

    Why: That is a volcano. Melted rock can come out of it.

  20. What is this shape of the Moon called?

    Choices: A crescent · A square · A full moon

    Answer

    A crescent

    Hint: It looks like a banana or a smile.

    Why: A thin curved Moon is called a crescent.

  21. When the whole Moon looks round and bright, what is it called?

    Choices: A full moon · A half moon · A new moon

    Answer

    A full moon

    Hint: It looks like a big round cookie.

    Why: A full round Moon is called a full moon.

Big: 1st to 3rd grade (23 questions)

  1. Which planet is closest to the Sun?

    Choices: Mars · Venus · Mercury

    Answer

    Mercury

    Hint: It is also the smallest planet.

    Why: Mercury is the closest planet to the Sun, and the smallest.

  2. Which planet that goes around the Sun is the biggest?

    Choices: Jupiter · Saturn · Neptune

    Answer

    Jupiter

    Hint: Look for a huge red storm spot on it.

    Why: Jupiter is the biggest planet. Its Great Red Spot is a huge storm.

  3. How many planets are in the solar system?

    Choices: Seven · Eight · Ten

    Answer

    Eight

    Hint: Pluto is now called a dwarf planet.

    Why: There are eight planets. Pluto is called a dwarf planet.

  4. How long does it take Earth to travel once around the Sun?

    Choices: One week · One day · About one year

    Answer

    About one year

    Hint: Think of birthdays.

    Why: Earth takes about one year to go around the Sun once.

  5. Earth spins all the way around once in about how long?

    Choices: One day · One year · One hour

    Answer

    One day

    Hint: From one sunrise to the next.

    Why: Earth spins once about every 24 hours. That spin gives day and night.

  6. What causes day and night on Earth?

    Choices: Clouds covering the Sun · The Sun turning off · Earth spinning

    Answer

    Earth spinning

    Hint: The planet turns like a top.

    Why: Earth spins. The side facing the Sun has day, and the side facing away has night.

  7. What is the hot, melted rock called while it is still underground?

    Choices: Magma · Sand · Lava

    Answer

    Magma

    Hint: Above ground it gets a different name.

    Why: Underground it is magma. When it flows out of a volcano, it is called lava.

  8. What kind of rock forms when lava or magma cools and hardens?

    Choices: Sedimentary rock · Metamorphic rock · Igneous rock

    Answer

    Igneous rock

    Hint: Ignis means fire.

    Why: Igneous rock forms from cooled melted rock. Granite and basalt are igneous.

  9. Rock made from layers of sand, mud and shells pressed together over time is...

    Choices: Sedimentary rock · Metamorphic rock · Igneous rock

    Answer

    Sedimentary rock

    Hint: Sediment is bits of sand and mud.

    Why: Sedimentary rock forms in layers. Sandstone and limestone are sedimentary, and fossils are often found in it.

  10. What is it called when wind and water slowly wear away rock and soil?

    Choices: Eruption · Evaporation · Erosion

    Answer

    Erosion

    Hint: It can carve canyons.

    Why: That is erosion. Over a very long time, a river can carve a deep canyon.

  11. Which is the largest ocean on Earth?

    Choices: The Atlantic Ocean · The Arctic Ocean · The Pacific Ocean

    Answer

    The Pacific Ocean

    Hint: It lies between Asia and the Americas.

    Why: The Pacific is the largest and deepest ocean.

  12. What do you call a huge mass of ice that creeps slowly downhill?

    Choices: A delta · A glacier · A geyser

    Answer

    A glacier

    Hint: It starts with G and moves very slowly.

    Why: A glacier is a huge mass of ice that slowly moves downhill.

  13. What is the name of the galaxy the solar system is in?

    Choices: Andromeda · The Milky Way · The Big Dipper

    Answer

    The Milky Way

    Hint: It sounds like a candy bar.

    Why: The solar system is in the Milky Way galaxy.

  14. When the Moon passes between the Sun and Earth and blocks the Sun, it is a...

    Choices: Lunar eclipse · Solar eclipse · Meteor shower

    Answer

    Solar eclipse

    Hint: Sol means Sun.

    Why: That is a solar eclipse. In a lunar eclipse, Earth's shadow falls on the Moon.

  15. What tool helps people see faraway stars and planets?

    Choices: A stethoscope · A telescope · A microscope

    Answer

    A telescope

    Hint: Tele means far.

    Why: A telescope makes faraway things look closer.

  16. Which star is close to straight above the North Pole and helps travelers find north?

    Choices: The North Star · The Sun · The Dog Star

    Answer

    The North Star

    Hint: Its other name is Polaris.

    Why: The North Star, Polaris, sits almost right above the North Pole, so it helps people find north.

  17. What do you call a hot spring that shoots water and steam high into the air?

    Choices: A geyser · A volcano · A glacier

    Answer

    A geyser

    Hint: Old Faithful in Yellowstone is one.

    Why: A geyser is a spring that sprays hot water and steam. Old Faithful is a famous one.

  18. What is the very center of Earth called?

    Choices: The mantle · The core · The crust

    Answer

    The core

    Hint: Like the center of an apple.

    Why: The center is the core. The outside layer is the crust, and the mantle is in between.

  19. What is the thin, rocky outer layer of Earth that people walk on?

    Choices: The mantle · The core · The crust

    Answer

    The crust

    Hint: Like the crust on bread.

    Why: The crust is Earth's outer layer. It is thin compared with the rest of the planet.

  20. Why does the Moon seem to change shape during a month?

    Choices: Different amounts of its sunlit side face Earth · It gets bitten by space rocks · Clouds cover parts of it

    Answer

    Different amounts of its sunlit side face Earth

    Hint: Half of the Moon is always lit by the Sun.

    Why: As the Moon goes around Earth, people see different amounts of its sunny side. Those are the phases.

  21. 🪐

    Name this planet, famous for its bright rings.

    Choices: Mercury · Mars · Saturn

    Answer

    Saturn

    Hint: Count out from the Sun: it is number six.

    Why: That is Saturn. Its rings are made of ice and rock.

  22. 🌍

    What covers most of the surface of this planet?

    Choices: Sand · Water · Ice cream

    Answer

    Water

    Hint: Look at all the blue.

    Why: Oceans cover most of Earth's surface.

  23. 🔭

    What do scientists look through to see faraway planets?

    Choices: A periscope · A telescope · A microscope

    Answer

    A telescope

    Hint: Tele means far.

    Why: A telescope makes faraway things look closer.
