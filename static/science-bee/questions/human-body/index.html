Human Body Science Bee Questions for Kids

The five senses, bones and muscles, heart and lungs, and how food gets used. 42 questions in two age bands, each with the answer, a gentle hint and a kind “here’s why” line.

Play a human body round Little: Pre-K–K Big: 1st–3rd

Read a question out loud and let the kids answer before you open the answer. Little kids: read the choices too.

Little: Pre-K to K (20 questions)

  1. What do you use to see?

    Choices: Your elbows · Your ears · Your eyes

    Answer

    Your eyes

    Hint: Blink blink.

    Why: You see with your eyes.

  2. What do you use to hear?

    Choices: Your knees · Your nose · Your ears

    Answer

    Your ears

    Hint: They are on the sides of your head.

    Why: You hear with your ears.

  3. What do you use to smell a flower?

    Choices: Your toes · Your nose · Your hair

    Answer

    Your nose

    Hint: Sniff sniff!

    Why: You smell with your nose.

  4. What part of your body pumps blood?

    Choices: Your hair · Your heart · Your teeth

    Answer

    Your heart

    Hint: Feel it go thump thump in your chest.

    Why: Your heart pumps blood all around your body.

  5. What do you use to breathe in air?

    Choices: Your lungs · Your stomach · Your feet

    Answer

    Your lungs

    Hint: They fill up like balloons in your chest.

    Why: Your lungs take in air when you breathe.

  6. What hard parts are inside your body and hold you up?

    Choices: Bones · Hair · Tears

    Answer

    Bones

    Hint: A skeleton is made of them.

    Why: Bones hold you up and make your skeleton.

  7. What should you do before you eat to wash away germs?

    Choices: Clap your hands · Hide your hands · Wash your hands

    Answer

    Wash your hands

    Hint: Use soap and water.

    Why: Washing your hands with soap and water helps wash germs away.

  8. What do you use to taste food?

    Choices: Your tongue · Your ears · Your elbow

    Answer

    Your tongue

    Hint: Stick it out!

    Why: Your tongue tastes sweet, salty, sour and more.

  9. What part of you does the thinking?

    Choices: Your belly button · Your brain · Your toes

    Answer

    Your brain

    Hint: It is inside your head.

    Why: Your brain does your thinking, remembering and dreaming.

  10. Where does the food you swallow go?

    Choices: To your ears · To your stomach · To your hair

    Answer

    To your stomach

    Hint: It is in your tummy.

    Why: Swallowed food goes down to your stomach to be broken down.

  11. What covers the outside of your whole body?

    Choices: Scales · Skin · Feathers

    Answer

    Skin

    Hint: You can feel touches with it.

    Why: Skin covers your body and lets you feel touch, warm and cold.

  12. What helps your body move, like when you lift your arm?

    Choices: Eyebrows · Fingernails · Muscles

    Answer

    Muscles

    Hint: Make a strong-arm pose!

    Why: Muscles pull on your bones to move your body.

  13. What protects your brain like a helmet?

    Choices: Your elbow · Your shoe · Your skull

    Answer

    Your skull

    Hint: It is the bone in your head.

    Why: Your skull is a strong bone that protects your brain.

  14. What do you use to chew food?

    Choices: Ears · Eyelashes · Teeth

    Answer

    Teeth

    Hint: Brush them twice a day.

    Why: You chew with your teeth. Brushing keeps them healthy.

  15. When you run fast, what happens to your heart?

    Choices: It beats faster · It falls asleep · It stops

    Answer

    It beats faster

    Hint: Put your hand on your chest after jumping.

    Why: Your heart beats faster when you exercise to send more blood to your muscles.

  16. Why do you cover your mouth when you sneeze?

    Choices: To make it louder · To keep germs from spreading · To catch a bug

    Answer

    To keep germs from spreading

    Hint: Achoo! Use your elbow.

    Why: Covering a sneeze keeps germs from flying onto other people.

  17. What does your body need every night to grow and rest?

    Choices: Television · Sleep · Candy

    Answer

    Sleep

    Hint: Close your eyes, good night!

    Why: Sleep helps your body rest, grow and get ready for a new day.

  18. What is the colored circle in your eye called?

    Choices: The nose · The chin · The iris

    Answer

    The iris

    Hint: Same name as a flower.

    Why: The colored part of your eye is the iris. It can be brown, blue, green and more.

  19. 👁️

    Which sense does this body part help with?

    Choices: Tasting · Seeing · Hearing

    Answer

    Seeing

    Hint: Look around!

    Why: Eyes help you see.

  20. 👂

    Which sense does this body part help with?

    Choices: Smelling · Hearing · Seeing

    Answer

    Hearing

    Hint: Listen!

    Why: Ears help you hear.

Big: 1st to 3rd grade (22 questions)

  1. Which organ is the largest one in the human body?

    Choices: Heart · Skin · Liver

    Answer

    Skin

    Hint: It is on the outside!

    Why: Skin is the body's largest organ.

  2. Which is the hardest material in the human body?

    Choices: Hair · Toenail · Tooth enamel

    Answer

    Tooth enamel

    Hint: It covers your teeth.

    Why: Tooth enamel, the outer layer of teeth, is the hardest material in the body.

  3. Which bone, up in your thigh, is the longest one you have?

    Choices: The finger bone · The thigh bone (femur) · The skull

    Answer

    The thigh bone (femur)

    Hint: It is in your upper leg.

    Why: The femur, your thigh bone, is longer than any other bone you have.

  4. What tubes carry blood around the body?

    Choices: Blood vessels · Tendons · Nerves

    Answer

    Blood vessels

    Hint: Veins and arteries are two kinds.

    Why: Blood vessels carry blood. Arteries carry it away from the heart and veins bring it back.

  5. Which bones make a cage that protects your heart and lungs?

    Choices: Kneecaps · Ribs · Toes

    Answer

    Ribs

    Hint: Feel them along your sides.

    Why: Your ribs form the rib cage, which protects your heart and lungs.

  6. What dark circle in the middle of your eye lets light in?

    Choices: The pupil · The eyebrow · The iris

    Answer

    The pupil

    Hint: It gets smaller in bright light.

    Why: The pupil lets light into the eye. It shrinks in bright light and widens in the dark.

  7. Which gas does your body need from the air you breathe in?

    Choices: Oxygen · Smoke · Helium

    Answer

    Oxygen

    Hint: Its symbol is O.

    Why: Your body needs oxygen. You breathe out carbon dioxide.

  8. Which gas do you breathe out?

    Choices: Helium · Carbon dioxide · Oxygen only

    Answer

    Carbon dioxide

    Hint: Plants use it to make food.

    Why: You breathe out carbon dioxide. Plants take it in to make their food.

  9. What liquid in your mouth starts breaking down food as you chew?

    Choices: Blood · Saliva · Tears

    Answer

    Saliva

    Hint: Another name is spit.

    Why: Saliva, or spit, starts breaking down food even before you swallow.

  10. Which blood cells help fight germs?

    Choices: White blood cells · Bone cells · Red blood cells

    Answer

    White blood cells

    Hint: They are named for a color, but not red.

    Why: White blood cells fight germs. Red blood cells carry oxygen.

  11. What do red blood cells carry around the body?

    Choices: Food crumbs · Oxygen · Sound

    Answer

    Oxygen

    Hint: It comes from your lungs.

    Why: Red blood cells carry oxygen from your lungs to the rest of your body.

  12. The heart is a special kind of what?

    Choices: Muscle · Bone · Hair

    Answer

    Muscle

    Hint: It squeezes all day and night.

    Why: The heart is a muscle that squeezes to pump blood.

  13. Which body system includes the brain, spinal cord and nerves?

    Choices: The skeleton · The nervous system · The digestive system

    Answer

    The nervous system

    Hint: It carries messages.

    Why: The nervous system carries messages between your brain and your body.

  14. Which body system breaks down food?

    Choices: The nervous system · The skeleton · The digestive system

    Answer

    The digestive system

    Hint: Mouth, stomach and intestines are part of it.

    Why: The digestive system breaks food down so your body can use it.

  15. What muscle under your lungs helps you breathe in?

    Choices: The diaphragm · The bicep · The heart

    Answer

    The diaphragm

    Hint: It starts with D and has a silent G.

    Why: The diaphragm is a dome-shaped muscle under your lungs that helps you breathe.

  16. What connects muscles to bones?

    Choices: Blood · Tendons · Fingernails

    Answer

    Tendons

    Hint: There is a big one at the back of your ankle.

    Why: Tendons connect muscles to bones, like the Achilles tendon at your heel.

  17. What do you call the places where two bones meet, like your knee and elbow?

    Choices: Joints · Muscles · Pupils

    Answer

    Joints

    Hint: They let you bend.

    Why: Joints are where bones meet. They let you bend and turn.

  18. Which sense uses the tiny bumps on your tongue called taste buds?

    Choices: Sight · Hearing · Taste

    Answer

    Taste

    Hint: Yum!

    Why: Taste buds on your tongue let you taste.

  19. Where in your body is the teeny bone called the stirrup?

    Choices: The nose · The ear · The foot

    Answer

    The ear

    Hint: It helps you hear.

    Why: The stirrup, or stapes, is a tiny bone inside the ear.

  20. What is the long tube that food passes through after the stomach, where most nutrients are taken in?

    Choices: The windpipe · The spine · The small intestine

    Answer

    The small intestine

    Hint: It is small in width but very long.

    Why: The small intestine takes in most nutrients from food. It is long and folded up in your belly.

  21. 🫀

    What does this organ do?

    Choices: Digests food · Pumps blood around the body · Helps you hear

    Answer

    Pumps blood around the body

    Hint: It goes thump thump.

    Why: The heart pumps blood all around your body.

  22. 🫁

    What do these two organs help you do?

    Choices: Breathe · Walk · See

    Answer

    Breathe

    Hint: They fill with air.

    Why: Your lungs help you breathe in oxygen and breathe out carbon dioxide.
