Weather Science Bee Questions for Kids

Rain, snow and rainbows, clouds, storms, seasons and the tools that measure weather. 43 questions in two age bands, each with the answer, a gentle hint and a kind “here’s why” line.

Play a weather round Little: Pre-K–K Big: 1st–3rd

Read a question out loud and let the kids answer before you open the answer. Little kids: read the choices too.

Little: Pre-K to K (21 questions)

  1. What falls from clouds on a rainy day?

    Choices: Rain · Sand · Leaves

    Answer

    Rain

    Hint: Bring an umbrella!

    Why: Rain is water drops falling from clouds.

  2. What cold, white flakes fall in winter in some places?

    Choices: Popcorn · Feathers · Snow

    Answer

    Snow

    Hint: You can build a snowman.

    Why: Snowflakes are made of ice and fall when it is very cold.

  3. What do you see in the sky after rain when the sun comes out?

    Choices: A rainbow · A rocket · A moon

    Answer

    A rainbow

    Hint: It has many colors.

    Why: A rainbow shows up when sunlight shines through raindrops.

  4. What makes a kite fly?

    Choices: Mud · Wind · Snow

    Answer

    Wind

    Hint: You cannot see it, but you can feel it.

    Why: Wind is moving air, and it lifts the kite.

  5. What loud boom comes after lightning?

    Choices: Music · A doorbell · Thunder

    Answer

    Thunder

    Hint: Rumble rumble.

    Why: Thunder is the sound lightning makes.

  6. What should you wear on a cold, snowy day?

    Choices: Sandals · A swimsuit · A warm coat

    Answer

    A warm coat

    Hint: Zip it up!

    Why: A warm coat keeps your body heat in on a cold day.

  7. What tool tells you how hot or cold it is?

    Choices: A ruler · A thermometer · A spoon

    Answer

    A thermometer

    Hint: Thermo means heat.

    Why: A thermometer measures temperature.

  8. Which season is usually the hottest?

    Choices: Winter · Summer

    Answer

    Summer

    Hint: Time for the pool!

    Why: Summer is usually the warmest season.

  9. In which season do many trees lose their leaves?

    Choices: Spring · Fall · Summer

    Answer

    Fall

    Hint: Its other name is autumn.

    Why: In fall, or autumn, many trees drop their colorful leaves.

  10. What are clouds made of?

    Choices: Tiny drops of water or ice · Smoke from chimneys · Cotton

    Answer

    Tiny drops of water or ice

    Hint: They look fluffy but they are wet.

    Why: Clouds are made of tiny water drops or tiny bits of ice floating in the air.

  11. Where is a safe place to be in a thunderstorm?

    Choices: In a pool · Inside a building · Under a tall tree

    Answer

    Inside a building

    Hint: Go in when thunder roars.

    Why: Go inside during a thunderstorm. Trees and water are not safe from lightning.

  12. What season comes after winter?

    Choices: Fall · Summer · Spring

    Answer

    Spring

    Hint: Flowers start to bloom.

    Why: After winter comes spring, when many plants start to grow again.

  13. What kind of weather is it when the sun shines and there are no clouds?

    Choices: Rainy · Snowy · Sunny

    Answer

    Sunny

    Hint: Wear a hat and sunscreen.

    Why: No clouds and lots of sun means sunny weather.

  14. What is a cloud that sits right on the ground called?

    Choices: Fog · Smoke · Hail

    Answer

    Fog

    Hint: It makes it hard to see far away.

    Why: Fog is a cloud close to the ground.

  15. Balls of ice that fall in some storms are called...

    Choices: Hail · Snowmen · Hay

    Answer

    Hail

    Hint: They can bounce on the ground.

    Why: Hail is lumps of ice that fall from some thunderstorms.

  16. What weather do you need to make puddles to jump in?

    Choices: Sunshine · Rain · Wind

    Answer

    Rain

    Hint: Splash!

    Why: Rain makes puddles.

  17. Which one helps you stay dry in the rain?

    Choices: An umbrella · A fan · A pillow

    Answer

    An umbrella

    Hint: You hold it over your head.

    Why: An umbrella keeps raindrops off you.

  18. What is a strong, spinning wind shaped like a funnel?

    Choices: A tornado · A rainbow · A breeze

    Answer

    A tornado

    Hint: It twists down from a storm cloud.

    Why: A tornado is a spinning column of air that reaches down from a storm.

  19. 🌈

    What two things help make this?

    Choices: Snow and the Moon · Wind and sand · Sunlight and raindrops

    Answer

    Sunlight and raindrops

    Hint: It can appear after a rain shower.

    Why: A rainbow appears when sunlight shines through raindrops.

  20. The red line on this thermometer is very high. Is it hot or cold?

    Choices: Cold · Hot

    Answer

    Hot

    Hint: High means warm.

    Why: A high red line means it is hot.

  21. ⛈️

    What loud sound comes after this flash?

    Choices: A song · Thunder · A whistle

    Answer

    Thunder

    Hint: Boom!

    Why: Thunder comes after lightning.

Big: 1st to 3rd grade (22 questions)

  1. What tool measures how fast the wind is blowing?

    Choices: An anemometer · A barometer · A thermometer

    Answer

    An anemometer

    Hint: It has little cups that spin.

    Why: An anemometer measures wind speed. Its cups spin faster in stronger wind.

  2. A weather vane tells you what about the wind?

    Choices: Its color · Its temperature · Its direction

    Answer

    Its direction

    Hint: It points like an arrow.

    Why: A weather vane shows which direction the wind is coming from.

  3. What tool collects rain so you can measure how much fell?

    Choices: A compass · A telescope · A rain gauge

    Answer

    A rain gauge

    Hint: Gauge means measure.

    Why: A rain gauge catches rain so you can see how much fell.

  4. What do you call water falling from clouds as rain, snow, sleet or hail?

    Choices: Precipitation · Condensation · Evaporation

    Answer

    Precipitation

    Hint: It starts with pre.

    Why: Any water falling from clouds is precipitation.

  5. In the water cycle, what happens right after water vapor cools high in the sky?

    Choices: It turns into rock · It disappears forever · It condenses into clouds

    Answer

    It condenses into clouds

    Hint: Remember drops on a cold glass.

    Why: Cooling vapor condenses into tiny drops that make clouds.

  6. What powers the water cycle by heating water so it evaporates?

    Choices: The Sun · The Moon · Earthquakes

    Answer

    The Sun

    Hint: It is the closest star.

    Why: The Sun's heat makes water evaporate and keeps the water cycle going.

  7. A huge spinning storm that forms over warm ocean water is a...

    Choices: Drought · Hurricane · Blizzard

    Answer

    Hurricane

    Hint: It has a calm eye in the middle.

    Why: A hurricane forms over warm ocean water and has a calm center called the eye.

  8. A long time with much less rain than usual is called a...

    Choices: Monsoon · Flood · Drought

    Answer

    Drought

    Hint: Plants dry up and the ground cracks.

    Why: A drought is a long dry spell with little rain.

  9. A big snowstorm with strong winds and blowing snow is a...

    Choices: Drizzle · Blizzard · Heat wave

    Answer

    Blizzard

    Hint: It makes it hard to see.

    Why: A blizzard is a snowstorm with strong winds.

  10. What kind of scientist studies and forecasts the weather?

    Choices: A meteorologist · A zoologist · A geologist

    Answer

    A meteorologist

    Hint: It has nothing to do with meteors!

    Why: A meteorologist studies the weather and makes forecasts.

  11. High, thin, wispy clouds made of ice crystals are called...

    Choices: Cumulus clouds · Cirrus clouds · Fog

    Answer

    Cirrus clouds

    Hint: They look like feathers or curls of hair.

    Why: Cirrus clouds are high, wispy and made of ice crystals.

  12. Puffy, white clouds that look like cotton balls are called...

    Choices: Cirrus clouds · Stratus clouds · Cumulus clouds

    Answer

    Cumulus clouds

    Hint: They are the ones you find shapes in.

    Why: Cumulus clouds are puffy and white, often on nice days.

  13. Tall, dark clouds that bring thunderstorms are called...

    Choices: Cumulonimbus clouds · Cirrus clouds · Contrails

    Answer

    Cumulonimbus clouds

    Hint: Nimbus means rain cloud.

    Why: Cumulonimbus clouds are tall storm clouds that bring thunder, lightning and heavy rain.

  14. What is the mix of gases around Earth that people breathe called?

    Choices: The crust · The atmosphere · The ocean

    Answer

    The atmosphere

    Hint: It is like a blanket of air around the planet.

    Why: The atmosphere is the layer of air around Earth.

  15. Why do you see lightning before you hear thunder?

    Choices: Light travels faster than sound · Thunder is shy · Lightning is closer

    Answer

    Light travels faster than sound

    Hint: Think about speed.

    Why: Light reaches you almost at once, but sound takes longer to travel.

  16. What causes the seasons on Earth?

    Choices: Earth's tilt as it goes around the Sun · The Moon blocking the Sun · Earth getting much closer and farther from the Sun

    Answer

    Earth's tilt as it goes around the Sun

    Hint: Earth leans a little to one side.

    Why: Earth is tilted. As it goes around the Sun, each half gets more direct sunlight for part of the year.

  17. Raindrops that freeze into little ice pellets before they reach the ground are called...

    Choices: Hail · Sleet · Dew

    Answer

    Sleet

    Hint: It rhymes with sheet.

    Why: Sleet is small ice pellets that freeze on the way down.

  18. Water drops that form on grass on a cool morning are called...

    Choices: Hail · Sleet · Dew

    Answer

    Dew

    Hint: It sounds like do.

    Why: Dew forms when water vapor in the air condenses on cool grass.

  19. What is the name for the weather a place usually has over many years?

    Choices: Breeze · Climate · Forecast

    Answer

    Climate

    Hint: A desert has a dry one.

    Why: Climate is the usual weather of a place over a long time. Weather is what happens today.

  20. What instrument measures air pressure to help forecast the weather?

    Choices: An anemometer · A barometer · A microscope

    Answer

    A barometer

    Hint: Baro means weight or pressure.

    Why: A barometer measures air pressure. Falling pressure can mean a storm is coming.

  21. 🌪️

    What kind of storm is this spinning funnel?

    Choices: A tornado · A hurricane · A blizzard

    Answer

    A tornado

    Hint: It reaches down from a thunderstorm to the ground.

    Why: That is a tornado, a spinning column of air from a thunderstorm.

  22. ❄️

    A snowflake is made of what?

    Choices: Salt · Ice crystals · Cotton

    Answer

    Ice crystals

    Hint: It is frozen water.

    Why: Snowflakes are made of ice crystals.
