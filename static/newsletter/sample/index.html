This week's animal: the red panda (it's not a bear)

Hi there,

This week's animal is the red panda. If your kid only knows the giant panda, this one is a fun surprise.

Red pandas live in mountain forests of the eastern Himalayas and southwestern China, and they spend a lot of time in trees. They have reddish-brown fur, a black belly and white face markings, plus a bushy tail with red and buff rings.

Quiz to do together. Read it aloud and let them guess.

1. Is a red panda a kind of bear, like a giant panda?

A. No, it is not a bear, and it is not closely related to the giant panda
B. Yes, it is a tiny bear in the same family as the giant panda
C. Yes, it is a baby giant panda that never grows up

2. What do red pandas mainly eat?

A. Only meat from big animals
B. Only fish from deep rivers
C. Mainly bamboo leaves and shoots

3. What special "thumb" helps a red panda hold bamboo?

A. A metal hook glued on at the zoo
B. Extra fingers that grow from its ears
C. A wrist bone that works like a thumb and helps it grip bamboo

Answers

1. A. Sharing the name "panda" does not make them close cousins.

2. C. They may also eat fruits and blossoms.

3. C. It's called a "false thumb": a wrist bone that helps it grip bamboo stalks.
