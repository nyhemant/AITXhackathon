Games for a zoo day

Short rounds for ages 5–7, on a phone in the car. A grown-up can read the lines aloud. No sign-in, and the cards keep working if the signal drops after they load.

Animal memory match

Flip two cards, find the twins, then hear a short fact from the animal card. Easy is 6 pairs; a bit harder is 8. About 1–3 minutes.

Animal word search

Slide across a name, or tap the first and last letter. Easy is a 6×6 grid; a bit harder is 8×8. About 1–3 minutes.

Who am I?

A grown-up reads two or three short clues. Then pick the animal. Six rounds, about 1–3 minutes.
